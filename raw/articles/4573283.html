NAVY

Integrity Technologies Corp.,* Grand Forks, North Dakota, is being awarded a $55,000,000,000 multiple award firm-fixed price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity Worldwide Expeditionary multiple award contract territorial integrity of the U.S. contract. The contract will provide supplies and services in support of but not limited to supporting the six phases of the continuum of military operations in support of the geographic Combatant Command's joint operations, coalition partners, and other U.S. federal agencies in setting and sustaining the theater through the following requirements: theater opening (includes reception, staging, onward-movement, and integration support); sustainment; theater distribution; and stability operations and Defense Support of Civil Authorities support. The description of contractual scope provides that services and supplies provided through the performance of this contract include but are not limited to humanitarian assistance/disaster relief, contingency, exercise, lodging, logistics, water-based and land-based support. The contract will run concurrently with other announced awardees under the contract. The contract includes a five-year base period of performance and a five-year option pursuant to Federal Acquisition Regulation 52.217-9, which if exercised will bring the total estimated value of the contract to $65,000,000,000. The base performance period for this new awardee will begin August 2026 and is expected to be completed by December 2029. If the five-year option is exercised, the performance period will be completed by December 2034. Work will be performed in the U.S. and outlying territories, but due to the fact that specific requirements cannot be predicted at this time, more specific information about the percentage of work and where it will be performed cannot be currently provided. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance funds in the contract's minimum amount of $500 will be obligated at time of award and those funds will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Additional funds will be obligated at the task order level with the appropriate fiscal year funding as issued by the customers for each area of responsibility. The requirement was competitively procured as a full and open competition with 36 offers received. Naval Supply Systems Command, Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania, is the contracting activity (N00023-26-D-0028).

Accenture Federal Services LLC, Arlington, Virginia (N6600126R0093); ActioNet Inc., Vienna, Virginia (N6600126R0094); Booz Allen Hamilton Inc., McLean, Virginia (N6600126R0095); CNF Technologies Corp., San Antonio, Texas (N6600126R0096); Cole Engineering Services Inc., Orlando, Florida (N6600126R0097); Core4ce LLC, Arlington, Virginia (N6600126R0098); CSA Global LLC, Virginia Beach, Virginia (N6600126R0099); Deloitte Consulting LLP, Arlington, Virginia (N6600126R0100); DLH LLC, Bethesda, Maryland (N6600126R0101); Dynamo Technologies LLC, Vienna, Virginia (N6600126R0102); Epsilon C5I Inc., San Diego, California (N6600126R0103); ExpediteInfoTech Inc., Rockville, Maryland (N6600126R0104); General Dynamics Information Technology Inc., Falls Church, Virginia (N6600126R0105); Highbury Defense Group, San Diego, California (N6600126R0106); Invictus International Consulting LLC, Alexandria, Virginia (N6600126R0107); LATG Inc., Metairie, Louisiana (N6600126R0108); Leidos Inc., Reston, Virginia (N6600126R0109); Maximus Federal Services Inc., McLean, Virginia (N6600126R0110); Parsons Government Services Inc., Centreville, Virginia (N6600126R0111); Quiet Professionals LLC, Tampa, Florida (N6600126R0112); REDLattice, Chantilly, Virginia (N6600126R0113); Resource Management Concepts Inc., Lexington Park, Maryland (N6600126R0114); Saalex Corp., Washington, D.C. (N6600126R0115); Science Applications International Corp., Reston, Virginia (N6600126R0116); Scientific Research Corp., Atlanta, Georgia (N6600126R0117); Sentar Inc., Huntsville, Alabama (N6600126R0118); Solute, San Diego, California (N6600126R0119); Tharros Defense Inc., Fulton, Maryland (N6600126R0120); and VSolvit LLC, Ventura, California (N6600126R0121), are awarded a $278,000,000 indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity, multiple award contract with cost-plus-fixed-fee and cost-no-fee pricing. The support provided under these contracts includes operational exercise design and construction, operations and requirements analysis, concept formulation and development, feasibility demonstrations, and operational and technical support. This includes efforts to analyze and engineer operational, functional and system requirements to establish national, theater, and force-level architecture. Additional efforts will include requirements verification and validation, engineering analysis, technical documentation, software and hardware design and implementation, as well as systems integration, test and evaluation, and demonstration, applying a systems engineering approach to development while ensuring secure development practices through DevSecOps methodologies. Work will be performed in San Diego, California. This seven-year contract includes one two-year option which, if exercised, would bring the potential value of this contract to $400,000,000. The period of performance is Aug. 12, 2026, through Aug. 12, 2031. Awardees will have the opportunity to compete for task orders during the ordering period. No funds will be obligated at the time of award; funds will be obligated on individual orders as they are issued. This contract was competitively procured via a request for proposal (N6600126R0001) published on the SAM.gov website and the Procurement Integrated Enterprise Environment (PIEE) Solicitation Module. 50 offers were received and twenty-nine were selected for award. Naval Information Warfare Center Pacific, San Diego, California, is the contracting activity.

Marine Hydraulics International LLC, Norfolk, Virginia, is awarded a $119,369,087 firm-fixed-price contract for maintenance, modernization, and repair of the USS San Antonio (LPD 17) during a fiscal 2027 selected restricted availability. The scope of this acquisition includes all labor, supervision, equipment, production, testing, facilities, and quality assurance necessary to prepare for and accomplish the Chief of Naval Operations availability for critical modernization, maintenance, and repair. This contract includes options, which, if exercised, would bring the cumulative value of this contract to $143,677,762. Work will be performed in Norfolk, Virginia, and is expected to be completed by July 2028. Fiscal 2026 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $104,625,213 (88%); fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Navy) funds in the amount of $14,706,036 (12%); and fiscal 2025 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $37,838 (<1%), will be obligated at time of award, of which $14,706,036 will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was competitively procured using full and open competition via the System for Award website, with four offers received. The Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity (N0002426C4417).

Conti Federal Services LLC, Orlando, Florida, is awarded a $77,981,311 firm-fixed-price contract for P-111, ordnance magazines, Sigonella, Italy. This contract constructs four earth-covered magazines with seismic upgrades, two modular storage magazines, and a maintenance facility for ammunition rework and overhaul shop. Work will be performed at Sigonella, Italy, and is expected to be complete by August 2030. Fiscal 2024 military construction funds in the amount of $67,685,727 will be obligated at time of award, and funds will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was competitively procured via the SAM.gov website, with four offers received. Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command Atlantic, Norfolk, Virginia, is the contracting activity (N62470-26-C-0005).

Hoffman Engineering LLC,* Stamford, Connecticut, is awarded a firm-fixed-price, not-to-exceed $10,000,000 indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract to procure replacement parts and provide repair, calibration, and upgrade to obsolete ANV-126A-001 computerized field portable night vision test sets to the current ANV-126A-3000 standard. This effort addresses the obsolescence of the ANV-126A-001 models and resolves ongoing sustainment issues caused by rising repair frequencies and the unavailability of legacy replacement components, ensuring the Navy's night vision diagnostic inventory remains fully operational. Work will be performed in Stamford, Connecticut, and is expected to be completed in August 2031. No funds will be obligated at the time of award; funds will be obligated on individual task orders as they are issued. This contract was not competitively procured. Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity (N0042126D0009).

ARMY

Northrop Grumman, Rolling Meadows, Illinois, was awarded a $158,831,128 modification (PZ0003) to contract W58RGZ-26-C-0009 for the definitization of the contract. Work will be performed in Rolling Meadows, Illinois, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 13, 2026. Fiscal 2026 revolving funds in the amount of $158,831,128 were obligated at the time of the award. Army Contracting Command, Redstone Arsenal, Alabama, is the contracting activity.

Washington Business Dynamics LLC,* Norman, Oklahoma, was awarded a $42,083,539 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for systems engineering and technical assistance staff support to the Product Director Virtual Training Systems, which develops, acquires and sustains virtual training solutions that enhance Army readiness. Bids were solicited via the internet with four received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 13, 2031. Army Contracting Command, Detroit Arsenal, Michigan, is the contracting activity (W912CH-26-D-A113).

Accenture Federal Services, Arlington, Virginia, was awarded a $24,251,590 firm-fixed-price contract for a six-month extension of contract W52P1J-21-C-0025 for the Enterprise Task Management Software Solution. Work will be performed at Fort Belvoir, Virginia, with an estimated completion date of Feb. 15, 2027. Fiscal 2026 operation and maintenance, Army funds in the amount of $18,818,938 were obligated at the time of award. Army Contracting Command, Rock Island Arsenal, Illinois, is the contracting activity (W519TC-26-C-A069).

Engineering Partners Inc.,* Hilo, Hawaii (W9128A-26-D-A019); InSynergy Engineering Inc.,* Honolulu, Hawaii (W9128A-26-D-A020); MK Engineers Ltd.,* Mililani, Hawaii (W9128A-26-D-A021); and Nakamura Oyama & Associates Inc.,* Honolulu, Hawaii (W9128A-26-D-A022), will compete for each order of the $9,900,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for electrical architect-engineer services to design miscellaneous projects. Bids were solicited via the internet with six received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 12, 2031. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Honolulu, Hawaii, is the contracting activity.

AIR FORCE

Parsons Government Services Inc., Centreville, Virginia, has been awarded a $15,139,840 cost-plus-fixed-fee contract for BLADE Battlefield Lethal AI-Driven Electronic Warfare. This contract provides for the design, development, and deployment of high value-added capabilities as they apply to electronic support and electronic attack to enable the engagement of new and emerging signals. Work will be performed at Chantilly, Centreville, and Herndon, Virginia, and is expected to be completed by Oct.13, 2030. This contract was a competitive acquisition and two offers were received. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $1,125,000 are being obligated at time of award. The Air Force Research Laboratory, Rome, New York, is the contracting activity (FA8750-26-C-B013). (Awarded on Aug 13, 2026.)

*Small business
