NAVY

Amee Bay LLC,* Anchorage, Alaska (N6449826D1021); Atlantic Fabrication & Boiler Services Inc.,* Portsmouth, Virginia (N6449826D1022); AVMAC LLC,* Chesapeake, Virginia (N6449826D1023); Continental Tide Defense Systems Inc.,* Wyomissing, Pennsylvania (N6449826D1024); Delphinus Engineering Inc.,* Newtown Square, Pennsylvania (N6449826D1025); Epsilon Systems Solutions Inc.,* Portsmouth, Virginia (N6449826D1026); La Playa Inc. of Virginia,* Chesapeake, Virginia (N6449826D1027); and Q.E.D. Systems Inc.,* Virginia Beach, Virginia (N6449826D1028), are awarded a combined $112,833,317 cost-plus-fixed-fee, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for hull, mechanical and electrical machinery and systems services in support of surface fleet, submarine, shore-based installations, other Federal Agency vessels, and select maritime agency vessels and land-based sites. Each awardee will be awarded $500 (minimum contract guarantee per awardee) at contract award. The contracts awarded to the eight vendors are not to exceed a cumulative value of $112,833,317 over a five-year ordering period. Work will be performed both in the continental U.S.; and overseas at various locations, including private shipyards, public Naval shipyards, military establishments, and contractor facilities. Work is expected to be completed by July 2031. Fiscal 2026 other procurement, (Navy) funding in the amount of $4,000 will be obligated at the time of award and will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. All other funding will be made available at the task order level as contracting actions occur. This contract was competitively procured utilizing full and open competition via the System for Award Management website, with eight offers received. The Naval Surface Warfare Center Philadelphia Division, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, is the contracting activity.

Core Tech-HDCC-Kajima (CHK) LLC, Tamuning, Guam, is awarded a $61,582,001 firm-fixed-price, unilateral modification to previously awarded contract (N62742-25-C-1319) for the construction of P-649, a joint communications upgrade, at Naval Base Guam and Camp Blaz. This award brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $351,344,615. The contract modification is for the exercise of Option Three for Fiber SONET Ring at Marine Corps Base Camp Blaz and Naval Base Guam. Work will be performed in Santa Rita, Guam; and Dededo, Guam, and is expected to be completed by March 2029. Fiscal 2026 military construction funds in the amount of $10,000,000 will be obligated at the time of award and funds will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. The Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command Marianas, Naval Support Activity Andersen, Guam, is the contracting activity (N62742-25-C-1319).

Agrippa Industries Inc.,* Palo Alto, California, is awarded an $18,997,203 firm-fixed-price contract for the Scalable Commercial Attritable Logistics Adapted to Expeditionary Systems effort. The project proposes to develop and eventually deploy a low-cost logistics solution to enable logistics at scale to support military operations. The maritime vessels will be adapted from Agrippa's commercial design and leverage existing Agrippa technology. This contract provides for the development of design modifications for the vessel, appropriate for the military mission. It will also develop launch, recovery, load, and offload capabilities. Work will be performed in Palo Alto, California, and is expected to be completed in November 2029. The total cumulative value of this contract, including a 39-month base period with no options, is $18,997,203. Fiscal 2025 research, development, test and evaluation (Navy) funds in the amount of $18,997,203 are obligated at the time of award and will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was competitively procured under long range broad agency announcement (BAA) (N0001425SB001) entitled "Long Range Broad Agency Announcement (BAA) for Navy and Marine Corps Science and Technology." Since proposals will be received throughout the year under the Long-Range BAA, the number of proposals received in response to the solicitation is unknown. The Office of Naval Research, Arlington, Virginia, is the contracting activity (N0001426C1315).

Northrup Grumman Systems Corp., Magna, Utah, is being awarded a $12,695,572 modification (P0000) to a previously awarded and announced contract (N0003026C2038) to provide services to complete capital maintenance projects and related capital maintenance studies. Projects completed as part of this effort are limited to non-severable improvements, repair, or modifications of Navy-owned land, buildings, and support utilities. Work will be performed in Magna, Utah. Work is expected to be completed April 1, 2030. Fiscal 2025 weapons procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $12,695,572 will be obligated on this modification. This contract was awarded as a sole source acquisition pursuant to 10 U.S. Code 2304(c)(1). Strategic Systems Programs, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity.

Nemean Solutions LLC,* Sierra Vista, Arizona, is awarded a $10,000,000 firm-fixed-price and cost reimbursable, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract to provide equipment specialist support services for the Program Manager, Intelligence and Cyberspace Operations. Work will be performed at the Marine Corps Logistics Base, Albany, Georgia, and is expected to be completed in August 2031. No funds will be obligated at time of award. Funds will be obligated on individual task orders as they are issued. This contract was not competitively procured pursuant to Federal Acquisition Regulation 6.302-5(b)(4). Portfolio Acquisition Executive-Marine Corps, Quantico, Virginia, is the contracting activity (M68754-26-D-0136).

U.S. SPECIAL OPERATIONS COMMAND

Blue Tech Inc., San Diego, California, is being awarded a delivery order (H9240326FE149) under NASA Solutions for Enterprise-Wide Procurement (SEWP). This is for a Cisco Security and Splunk 3.0 Enterprise Agreement with a 12-month base year and four 12-month option periods. This firm-fixed-price delivery order under NASA SEWP Contract Number NNG15SD00B has a total contract value of $68,973,746.85. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $13,310,169 are obligated on at time of award. The primary performance location is U.S. Special Operations Command, MacDill Air Force Base, Florida.

DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY

Kelvin Hughes LLC, doing business as Hensoldt North America, Bethesda, Maryland, has been awarded a maximum $57,300,000 firm-fixed-price contract for Eyesafe Laser Rangefinders with containers. This was a sole-source acquisition using justification 10 U.S. Code 3204 (a)(1), as stated in Federal Acquisition Regulation 6.302-1. This is five-year contract with no option periods. The performance completion date is Aug. 16, 2032. Using military service is Army. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 Army working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Warren, Michigan (SPRDL1-26-D-0061).

Bimbo Bakeries USA Inc., Irving, Texas, has been awarded a maximum $26,100,000 fixed price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for fresh bread and bakery items. This was a competitive acquisition with two responses received. This is a three-year contract with no option periods. Location of performance is Texas and Oklahoma, with an Oct. 6, 2029, ordering period end date. Using customers are Coast Guard, Army, and Air Force. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2029 defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Troop Support, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania (SPE300-26-D-W009).

UPDATE: Mercy Medical Equipment Co.,* San Antonio, Texas (SPE2DH-26-D-0017, $16,500,000), has been added as an awardee to the multiple award contract for medical equipment and accessories for the Defense Logistics Agency Electronic Catalog, issued against solicitation SPE2DH-21-R-0002 and awarded Feb. 10, 2022. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Troop Support, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

CORRECTION: The contract announced on Aug. 12, 2026, for US Foods Inc., doing business as US Food Service Manassas, Manassas, Virginia (SPE300-26-D-3021), for $69,000,000 was announced with an incorrect award date. The correct award date is Aug. 13, 2026. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Troop Support, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

CORRECTION: The contract announced on Aug. 14, 2026, for Oshkosh Defense LLC, Oshkosh, Wisconsin (SPRDL1-26-D-0053), for $19,490,398 was announced with an incorrect award date. The correct award date is Aug. 18, 2026. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Warren, Michigan (SPRDL1-26-D-0053).

AIR FORCE

Honeywell International Inc., Phoenix, Arizona, has been awarded a $19,814,712 cost share contract, for the acquisition of the Aggressive Embedded Propulsion Operability. This contract provides for development of advanced computational tools and processes for designing a fan module in an embedded propulsion installation. The location of performance is Phoenix, Arizona. The work is expected to be completed by Oct. 18, 2030. This award is the result of a competitive acquisition and one solicitation mailed, and four offers received. Fiscal 2025 research and development funds in the amount of $10,166,929 are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Research Lab, Air Warfare Directorate, Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio, is the contracting activity (FA2391-26-B-B027). (Awarded on Aug. 17, 2026).

Trusted Space LLC, Leesburg, Virginia, has been awarded a $14,831,233 ceiling, cost-plus-fixed-fee contract for the Resilient and Autonomous Satellite Experimental Research program. This contract provides for the research, development, software maturation, testing, and on-orbit demonstration of autonomy software prototypes to build trust, safety, and resilience into multi-agent autonomous space systems. Work will be performed at Leesburg, Virginia; and Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico, and is expected to be completed by Aug. 19, 2031. This contract was a competitive acquisition and one offer was received. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $516,346 are being obligated at time of award. The Air Force Research Laboratory, Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico, is the contracting activity (FA9453-26-C-X018). (Awarded on Aug.18, 2026).

Language Associates LLC, doing business as Diplomatic Language Services, Arlington, Virginia, was awarded a $13,000,000 maximum $11,748,457 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for English language training. This contract provides for English language instructors to support the Defense Language Institute English Language Center Language Training Detachment in the United Arab Emirates. Work will be performed in Dubai, UAE, and is expected to be completed by Sept. 20, 2029. This contract involves Foreign Military Sales (FMS) funds. This contract was a sole source acquisition. FMS funds in the amount of $3,797,902 are being obligated at time of award. The 338th Enterprise Sourcing Squadron, Joint Base San Antonio Randolph Texas, is the contracting activity (FA300226D0029).

CORRECTION: The contract modification announced on Aug. 14, 2026, for The Boeing Co., Tukwila, Washington, for a maximum $75,000,000 modification (P00187) to a previously awarded contract (FA8625-16-C-6599) was announced with the wrong award date. The correct award date is Aug 18, 2026.

ARMY

Accenture Federal Services LLC, Arlington, Virginia, was awarded a $13,578,638 firm-fixed-price contract to provide a commercial-managed service solution to automate cloud-onboarding, governance, and financial operations. The amount of this action is $13,578,638. Bids were solicited via the internet with 14 received. Work will be performed in Arlington, Virginia, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 18, 2029. Fiscal 2010 operation and maintenance, Army funds in the amount of $2,000,000 were obligated at the time of the award. Army Contracting Command, Rock Island, Illinois, is the contracting activity (W519TC-25-C-A062).

Applied Technologies Group Inc.,* Huntsville, Alabama, was awarded a $12,948,210 modification (P00047) to contract W31P4Q-23-F-E001 for logistics support for the Utility Helicopters Project Office. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $65,283,922. Bids were solicited via the internet with four received. Work will be performed in Huntsville, Alabama, with an estimated completion date of Oct. 12, 2027. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test, and evaluation, Army funds; fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance, Army funds; 2026 Foreign Military Sales administration funds; and 2010 Foreign Military Sales (Albania, Ashmore and Cartier Islands, Austria, Bahrain, Brazil, Comoros, Croatia, Greece, Israel, Japan, Jordan, Latvia, Lithuania, Mexico, Slovakia, Slovenia, South Korea, Sweden, Taiwan, Thailand, Turkey, United Arab Emirates) funds in the amount of $12,948,210, were obligated at the time of the award. Army Contracting Command, Redstone Arsenal, Alabama, is the contracting activity.

Great Lakes Dredge & Dock Co. LLC, Houston, Texas, was awarded a $12,275,250 modification (P00007) to contract W9127S-22-D-0026 for long-term capability, station dredging on the McClellan-Kerr Arkansas River Navigation System. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $59,134,410. Bids were solicited via the internet with two received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 20, 2027. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Little Rock, Arkansas, is the contracting activity (W9127S-22-D-0026).

Tern AI Inc., Austin, Texas, was awarded an $11,263,793 firm-fixed-price contract under a Phase III Small Business Innovation Research award to provide an Independently Derived Positioning System and vehicle health & maintenance system. One bid was solicited with one received. Work will be performed in Austin, Texas, with an estimated completion date of August 19, 2027. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test, and evaluation, Army funds in the amount of $11,263,793 were obligated at the time of the award. U.S. Army FUZE Contracting Center, Arlington, Virginia, is the contracting activity (W51701-26-C-A205).

Downrange Operations and Training LLC,* Laramie, Wyoming, was awarded a $9,455,466 modification (P00005) to contract W900KK-25-F-0024 for training, technical support, and advice for THAAD missile air defense firing units. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $24,358,729. Bids were solicited via the internet with two received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Dec. 9, 2027. U.S. Army Contracting Command, Orlando, Florida, is the contracting activity (W900KK-25-F-0024).

*Small business
