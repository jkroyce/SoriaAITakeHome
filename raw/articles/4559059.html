NAVY

General Dynamics Electric Boat Corp., Groton, Connecticut (N00024-12-C-2115, N00024-17-C-2100, N00024-17-C-2117); and Huntington Ingalls Inc., Newport News Shipbuilding, Newport News, Virginia (N00024-17-C-2100, N00024-15-C-2114, N00024-16-C-2116), are awarded incentive contract modifications for the construction of five Columbia-class fleet ballistic missile submarines SSBN 828-832 (Build II), the construction of nine Virginia-class attack submarines SSN 814-822 (Block VI), and a tenth shipset of material; and investments in shipbuilder productivity efforts. The Block VI award includes previously announced material awards (including long-lead-time material and economic ordering quantity material). The announcement also includes previously awarded investments in shipbuilder productivity efforts. Work will be performed in Groton, Connecticut (30%); Newport News, Virginia (22%); Quonset Point, Rhode Island (18%); Sunnyvale, California (5%); Sykesville, Maryland (1%); Goose Creek, South Carolina (1%); Chesapeake, Virginia (1%); Minneapolis, Minnesota (1%); Tucson, Arizona (1%); and other locations less than 1% (20%) and is expected to be completed by July 2038. Fiscal 2026 shipbuilding and conversion (Navy) funding in the amount of $6,441,442,332 (54%); fiscal 2025 shipbuilding and conversion (Navy) funding in the amount of $3,744,758,492 (32%); fiscal 2025 maritime industrial base funds in the amount of $1,266,948,874 (11%); fiscal 2026 national sea-based deterrence fund funds in the amount of $253,954,000 (2%); and fiscal 2024 shipbuilding and conversion (Navy) funding in the amount of $171,096,970 (1%), will be obligated at the time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity.

DZSP 21 LLC, Chantilly, Virginia, was awarded a $138,004,329 cost-plus-award-fee modification to previously awarded contract for base operations support services, Joint Region Marianas, Guam. This award brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $908,108,425. This contract modification is for the exercise of option period six. Work will be performed at various Navy, Marine Corps, and Air Force installations in Joint Region Marianas, Guam, and is expected to be completed by July 2027. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (O&M) (Navy); fiscal 2026 O&M (Marine Corps); and fiscal 2026 O&M (Defense); funds in the amount of $32,726,022, will be obligated at time of award, and funds will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command, Marianas, Guam, is the contracting activity (N40192-25-C-5400).

WASHINGTON HEADQUARTERS SERVICES

Applied Environmental Inc, Herndon, Virginia, has been awarded a firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract (HQ003426DE019) with a value of $50,000,000. The purpose of this contract is to provide environmental, planning, and resiliency technical support. Support efforts also include but are not limited to multimedia environmental compliance, resiliency, incident response and recovery, natural resources, and cultural resources. Work will be performed at the Pentagon, Arlington, Virginia; the Mark Center, Alexandria, Virginia; and Department of Defense facilities in Fairfield, Pennsylvania; and Falls Church, Virginia. The estimated completion date is Jan. 31, 2032. The total amount of the contract if all options are exercised is $50,0000,000. Funds in the amount of $2,5000,000, are being obligated at the time of the award. Washington Headquarters Services, Arlington, Virginia, is the contracting activity.

DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY

Kandor Manufacturing Inc.,* Arecibo, Puerto Rico, has been awarded a maximum $33,785,445 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for green service uniform dress shirts. This was a competitive acquisition with seven responses received. This is a five-year contract with no option periods. The ordering period end date is July 27, 2031. Using military service is Army. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2031 defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Troop Support, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania (SPE1C1-26-D-0069).

UPDATE: Nobel Biocare USA LLC, Brea, California (SPE2DF-26-D-0016, $48,000,000), has been added as an awardee to the multiple award contract for dental supplies and equipment for the Defense Logistics Agency Electronic Catalog, issued against solicitation SPE2DE-25-R-0002 and awarded Feb. 12, 2026. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Troop Support, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

AIR FORCE

Impulse Space Inc., Redondo Beach, California, has been awarded a $27,770,000 Small Business Innovation Research Phase III contract modification (P00010) to previously awarded contract (FA8809-25-C-B001) for two additional Mira space vehicles. Government-furnished payloads hosted on these vehicles will perform space domain awareness characterization in low Earth orbit. This modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $62,808,900 from $35,038,900. Work will be performed in Redondo Beach, California, and is expected to be completed by March 31, 2030. Fiscal 2025 procurement funds in the amount of $19,750,000 are being obligated at time of award. Space Systems Command, Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico, is the contracting activity. (Awarded July 28, 2026).

Protagonist Technology LLC, Washington, D.C., has been awarded a $13,761,917 modification (P00004) to previously awarded firm-fixed price contract FA8802-26-C-0003 for Small Business Innovation Research Phase III Support. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $20,503,757 from $6,741,840. Work will be performed in Washington, D.C., and is expected to be completed by Feb. 17, 2030. Fiscal 2025 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $13,761,917 are being obligated at time of award. This award is the result of a sole source acquisition. The Enterprise Sourcing Division, Los Angeles Air Force Base, California, is the contracting activity. (Awarded July 28, 2026.).

*Small business
