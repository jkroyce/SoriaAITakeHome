ARMY

Brasfield & Gorrie LLC, Birmingham, Alabama, was awarded a $658,173,000 contract with a total cumulative face value of $986,914,726, for the design and construction of four separate facilities for component remanufacture, missile maintenance, paint, and storage. Bids were solicited via the internet with two received. Work will be performed in Anniston, Alabama, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 30, 2030. Fiscal 2026 military construction, Army funds in the amount of $658,173,000 were obligated at the time of award. Army Mission Installation Contracting Command, Fort Sam Houston, Texas, is the contracting activity (W91QEY-26-9-A004).

Heaviside Industries Inc., Marina del Rey, California, was awarded a $99,000,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for the procurement, fielding, upgrades, and sustainment of the Heaviside MOTH spectrum analyzer. Bids were solicited via the internet with 99 received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 27, 2031. Army Contracting Command, Aberdeen Proving Ground, Maryland, is the contracting activity (W56KGY-26-D-A003).

Conti Federal Services LLC, Orlando, Florida, was awarded a firm-fixed-price contract for construction of a special tactics complex for the Oregon Air National Guard. The amount of this action is $55,432,141 with a total cumulative face value of $66,384,447. Bids were solicited via the internet with two received. Work will be performed in Portland, Oregon, with an estimated completion date of May 3, 2029. Fiscal 2023 and 2024 military construction, defense-wide funds in the amount of $55,432,141 were obligated at the time of award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Seattle, Washington, is the contracting activity (W912DW-26-C-A030).

Foster-Miller Inc. doing business as QinetiQ North America, Waltham, Massachusetts, was awarded a $36,448,495 cost-plus-fixed-fee, firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract to provide sustainment and maintenance services for postproduction software sustainment of the Common Robotic System - Individual. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 20, 2033. Army Contracting Command, Detroit Arsenal, Michigan, is the contracting activity (W912CH-26-D-A109).

Elbit America Inc., Talladega, Alabama, was awarded a $34,120,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract to provide repair services and materials for integrated helmet and display sight system components for AH-64D and AH-64E Apache attack helicopters. One bid was solicited with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 29, 2031. Army Contracting Command, Detroit Arsenal, Michigan, is the contracting activity (W912CH-26-D-0059).

ACC Construction Co. Inc., Augusta, Georgia, was awarded a firm-fixed-price contract for construction of a new hangar, shops, storage, and administration building with functional spaces and supporting building systems. The amount of this action is $73,984,082 with a total cumulative face value of $81,544,927. Bids were solicited via the internet with four received. Work will be performed in Fort Bragg, North Carolina, with an estimated completion date of Jan. 7, 2030. Fiscal 2026 military construction, defense-wide funds in the amount of $73,984,082 were obligated at the time of the award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Wilmington, North Carolina, is the contracting activity (W912PM-26-C-A020).

Raytheon Co., Huntsville, Alabama, was awarded a $38,378,404 cost-no-fee, cost-plus-fixed-fee, firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract to maintain specialized training software and procure Plug and Fight A-Kit hardware. One bid was solicited with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 17, 2027. Army Contracting Command, Redstone Arsenal, Alabama, is the contracting activity (W31P4Q-26-F-0040). (Awarded Aug. 17, 2026).

Integrate DG LLC,* Stamford, Connecticut, was awarded a $27,827,350 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract to procure Alphawing uncrewed aircraft systems, support equipment, and field service representatives for system use and employment. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Oct. 30, 2026. Army Contracting Command, Redstone Arsenal, Alabama, is the contracting activity (W58RGZ-26-F-0403).

Emerald Energy & Exploration Land Co.,* Corbin, Kentucky, was awarded a $19,000,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for real estate title services. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 26, 2031. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Fort Worth, Texas, is the contracting activity (W9126G-26-D-A058).

Folsom Dam Constructors, Pleasanton, California, was awarded a $10,315,577 modification (P00022) to contract W91238-23-C-0010 for Folsom Dam Wing Dam construction. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $136,038,717. Work will be performed in Folsom, California, with an estimated completion date of Oct. 26, 2029. Fiscal 2026 civil construction funds in the amount of $10,315,577 were obligated at the time of the award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Sacramento, California, is the contracting activity.

General Dynamics Land Systems Customer Service and Support Co., Sterling Heights, Michigan, was awarded a $13,029,776 modification (P00027) to contract W56HZV-23-C-0059 to operate and maintain a government facility and provide repair and sustainment services for the M1A1/M1A2 Abrams and M2A2/M3A3 Bradley family of vehicles. Work will be performed in Fort Hood, Texas, with an estimated completion date of March 31, 2027. Fiscal 2026 revolving funds in the amount of $13,029,776 were obligated at the time of the award. Army Contracting Command, Detroit Arsenal, Michigan, is the contracting activity.

NAVY

1080 Constructors, Omaha, Nebraska, is awarded a $654,316,365 firm-fixed-price with economic-price-adjustment contract for P1080, Power Reliability and Water Resiliency Upgrades, at Portsmouth Naval Shipyard. The maximum dollar value, including unexercised options, is $682,383,174. Work will be performed at Kittery, Maine, and is expected to be completed by December 2031. Fiscal 2026 military construction (Navy) funds in the amount of $166,894,326 will be obligated at time of award, with future increments funded with future year military construction (Navy) funds. This contract was competitively procured via SAM.gov, with three offers received. Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command, Atlantic, Norfolk Virginia, is the contracting activity (N62470-26-C-0007).

RTX Corp., Pratt & Whitney Military Engines, East Hartford, Connecticut, is awarded a not-to-exceed $240,754,035 cost-plus-incentive-fee and cost-plus-fixed-fee modification (P00013) to previously awarded contract (N0001924C0042). This modification increases the not-to-exceed value, adding scope to provide non-recurring engineering to mature the F135 engine, address operational issues, improve readiness, and support the transition of the engine core upgrade from risk reduction design review into engineering and manufacturing development, including long-lead hardware for a spare test engine in support of the Air Force, Marine Corps, Navy, and F-35 Cooperative Program Partners F-35 Joint Strike Fighter programs. Work will be performed in East Hartford, Connecticut (53%); Indianapolis, Indiana (15%); Middletown, Connecticut (7%); West Palm Beach, Florida (7%); Windsor Locks, Connecticut (6%); North Berwick, Maine (5%); Rockford, Illinois (4%); and Aguadilla, Puerto Rico (3%), and is expected to be completed in March 2028. Fiscal 2025 research, development, test and evaluation (Air Force) funds in the amount of $87,574,189; fiscal 2025 research, development, test and evaluation (Navy) funds in the amount of $87,574,190; and F-35 cooperative program partner funds in the amount of $39,008,976, will be obligated at the time of award. This contract was not competitively procured. Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

Lockheed Martin Corp., Orlando, Florida, is awarded a $48,965,977 cost-plus-fixed-fee and cost-only indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for contractor support services, interim sustainment support, and engineering support associated with the Target Sight System. Work will be performed in Orlando, Florida, and is expected to be completed by August 2031. Fiscal 2026 aircraft procurement, Navy funds in the amount of $3,500,000 will be obligated at time of award and not expire at the end of the fiscal year. This contract was not competitively procured via the System for Award Management website. Contract (N0016426DLN08) is a sole-source award under 10 U.S. Code 3204(a)(1) only one responsible source and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements. The Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane, Crane, Indiana, is the contracting activity (N0016426DLN08).

General Dynamics Mission Systems, Pittsfield, Massachusetts, is awarded a $31,769,754 cost-plus-incentive-fee and cost only modification to previously awarded contract (N00024-20-C-5603) to exercise options for the sustainment of the Littoral Combat Ship Integrated Combat Management System and associated Combat System elements. Work will be performed in Pittsfield, Massachusetts (85%); and San Diego, California (15%), and is expected to be completed by August 2027. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Navy) funds in the amount of $1,552,836 (61%); and fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation (Navy) funds in the amount of $1,000,000 (39%), will be obligated at the time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. The Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity.

SyQwest LLC, Cranston, Rhode Island, is awarded a $14,113,234 firm-fixed-price and cost-plus-fixed-fee contract for the production, testing, and delivery of source and sensor kits to be used on the MK48 heavyweight torpedo, a submarine launched anti-submarine and anti-surface warfare weapon. This contract includes options which, if exercised, would bring the cumulative value of this contract to $92,443,831. Work will be performed in Cranston, Rhode Island, and is expected to be completed by July 2027. If all options are exercised, work will continue through July 2032. Fiscal 2025 weapons procurement, Navy funds in the amount of $11,359,795 (80%); and Special Deposits funds in the amount of $2,753,439 (20%), and will be obligated at time of award. This contract was not competitively procured in accordance with 10 U.S. Code 4022 and Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement 206.001-70 as a direct follow-on to a competitively awarded Other Transaction Agreement. The Naval Undersea Warfare Center Division Newport, Newport, Rhode Island, is the contracting activity (N66604-26-C-0200).

Ma'at Enterprises Inc., Charlotte, North Carolina, is awarded a $10,656,847 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract (M00263-26-D-R003) for Salesforce training services. This contract provides for developed instruction, instructional reviews, video content development, technical writing, facilitation, and monthly alignment meetings in support of the Marine Corps Recruiting Command. Work will be performed at Charlotte, North Carolina, with an expected completion date of Aug. 23, 2031. This contract includes a five-year ordering period and does not include options. Fiscal 2026 operation and maintenance (Marine Corps) funds in the amount of $139,149 are being obligated at the time of award, via written task order, and will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was competitively procured via the SAM.gov website with five proposals received. The Regional Contracting Office, Parris Island, South Carolina, is the contracting activity.

AIR FORCE

World Wide Technology LLC, Maryland Heights, Missouri, was awarded a ceiling $99,000,000 firm-fixed-price contract for 688 Cyberspace Wing Cybersecurity Service Provider-Assurance, Risk, Governance, and Innovation Systems Security. This contract provides for professional cybersecurity and engineering services. Work will be performed at Joint Base-San Antonio Lackland, Texas and is expected to be completed by Sept. 14, 2031. This contract was a competitive acquisition and 22 offers were received. No funds are being obligated at time of award. The Air Combat Command, Acquisition Management and Integration Center, Tinker Air Force Base, Oklahoma, is the contracting activity (FA877326D0004).

Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., McLean, Virginia, has been awarded a $71,927,435 engineering change proposal modification (P00060) to previously awarded contract FA8750-23-F-0501 for InSITE software and hardware prototypes, InSITE capability procurement, fielding and installation with support, and InSITE engineering services. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the order to $452,936,693 from $381,009,258. Work will be performed at McLean, Virginia, and is expected to be completed by Dec. 3, 2027. Fiscal 2025 research, development, test, and evaluation funds in the amount of $3,150,000, and Fiscal 2026 operation and maintenance funds in the amount of $3,972,836 are being obligated at time of award. The Air Force Research Laboratory, Computing, Communication, and Information Systems Contracting Branch, Rome, New York, is the contracting activity.

Aegis Aerospace Inc., Webster, Texas, has been awarded a $24,964,741 cost-plus-fixed-fee task order against a previously issued indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract (80JSC024DA002). This task order provides services to support suborbital missions and Department of War payloads on government, commercial, and international launch vehicles and hosting platforms. Work will be performed in Houston, Texas, and is expected to be complete by Sept. 30, 2034. This contract was a competitive acquisition and four offers were received. Fiscal 2026 research and development funds in the amount of $550,000 are being obligated at time of award. The Space Systems Command, Kirtland, Air Force Base, Albuquerque, New Mexico, is the contracting activity (FA8809-26-F-B003).

L3Harris Technologies Inc., Rochester, New York, has been awarded a $12,305,752 cost-plus-fixed-fee contract for advanced processing and exploitation software and reports. This contract provides for the Modality Independent Raster Image (MIRI) technology and demonstrates MIRI as an emerging capability within the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency PHENOM environment. Work will be performed at Rochester, New York and Herndon, Virginia, and is expected to be completed by Oct. 27, 2028. This contract was a competitive acquisition and two offers were received. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $2,000,000 are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Research Laboratory, Contracting Office, Rome New York, is the contracting activity (FA8750-26-C-B044).

DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY

Blind Industries and Services of Maryland,** Baltimore, Maryland, has been awarded a maximum $49,267,006 firm-fixed-price with economic-price-adjustment, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for wind jackets. This is a five-year contract with no option periods. The ordering period end date is Aug. 26, 2031. Using military service is Army. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2031 defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Troop Support, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania (SPE1C1-26-D-B014).

CFM International Inc., West Chester, Ohio, has been awarded an estimated $10,344,275.00 firm-fixed-price delivery order (SPRTA1-26-F-0553) issued against a five-year basic ordering agreement (SPE4A1-26-G-0012) with no option periods for the manufacture of F108 Aircraft fan blade sets. The performance completion date is Nov. 28, 2028. Using military service is Air Force. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Tinker Air Force Base, Oklahoma.

DEFENSE ADVANCED RESEARCH PROJECTS AGENCY

Guidehouse Inc., McLean, Virginia, has been awarded a $25,335,837 hybrid, firm-fixed-price, time-and-materials, labor-hour task order for operations support services. This task order will specifically support the development, implementation, and maintenance of critical support functions, including facilities management, human resources and recruiting, visual media, compliance, financial management, and travel support. The location of performance is Arlington, Virginia. The work is expected to be completed by September 2027. This award is the result of a competitive acquisition under General Services Administration's One Acquisition Solution for Integrated Services Plus and two offers were received. Fiscal 2026 research and development funds in the amount of $6,327,825 are being obligated at the time of award. The Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency, Arlington, Virginia, is the contracting activity (HR001126FE079).
