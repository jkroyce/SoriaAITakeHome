AIR FORCE

All Points Logistics, Merritt Island, Florida, was awarded a $250,000,000 firm-fixed-price contract for space vehicle processing. This contract provides for space vehicle processing facilities. Work will be performed at Vandenberg Space Force Base, California, and is expected to be completed by July 30, 2029. This contract was a competitive acquisition and four offers were received. The Space Systems Command, Los Angeles Air Force Base, California, is the contracting activity (FA8802-26-C-0005).

Vivsoft Technologies LLC, Herndon, Virginia, was awarded a $100,000,000 firm fixed price other transaction agreement for the Aerospace Readiness Enterprise System requirement. This agreement provides for modernization of the Department’s aircrew scheduling, training management, and standardization and evaluation capabilities. The location of performance is Herndon, Virginia, and is expected to be complete by July 22, 2030. This contract was a competitive acquisition and three offers were received. Fiscal 2026 research and development funds in the amount of $3,530,268 are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Life Cycle Management Center, Hanscom Air Force Base, Massachusetts, is the contracting activity (FA8730-26-9-B004). (Awarded on July 23, 2024).

NAVY

The Boeing Co., Seattle, Washington, is awarded a not-to-exceed $213,000,000 modification (P00010) to a cost-plus-fixed-fee undefinitized contract (N0001924C0025). This modification adds scope to procure non-recurring engineering to support reconfiguration of four P-8A Multi-mission Maritime aircraft previously procured by a Foreign Military Sales (FMS) customer. This effort will modify the aircraft configuration to meet the customer's specific operational requirements. Work will be performed in Seattle, Washington (91%) and Huntington Beach, California (9%) and is expected to be completed by December 2031. FMS customer funds in the amount of $74,895,757 will be obligated at the time of award. This contract was not competed. Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

DRS Laurel Technologies Partnership, Largo, Florida, was awarded a $68,322,385 firm-fixed-price modification to a previously awarded contract (N6339423C0006) for the manufacturing, assembling, testing, and delivery of Vertical Launching System associated kits. This contract combines purchases for the U.S. government (87%); and the government of the Netherlands (13%), under the Foreign Military Sales (FMS) Program. Work will be performed in Largo, Florida, and is expected to be completed by July 2027. Fiscal 2026 procurement (Defense-wide) funds in the amount of $41,797,353 (61%); fiscal 2026 shipbuilding and conversion (Navy) funds in the amount of $16,973,361 (25%); FMS (Netherlands) funds in the amount of $8,630,986 (12%); fiscal 2026 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $592,701 (1%); and fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation (Defense-wide) funds in the amount of $327,984 (1%), funding will be obligated at time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Naval Surface Warfare Center Port Hueneme Division, Port Hueneme, California, is the contracting activity. (Awarded July 23, 2026).

Compass Systems Inc.,* Johnstown, Pennsylvania, is awarded a $49,896,823 cost-plus-fixed-fee order (N6833526F1132) against a previously issued basic ordering agreement (N6833526G1010). This is a Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) Phase III effort for Topic N201-015 entitled, "Autonomous and Intelligent Aircraft Maintenance Technologies" derived from the Phase I and II efforts and is to transition proven Phase II technologies from prototype demonstration to operational deployment across multiple Fleet Readiness Centers (FRC). The work remains within the original SBIR scope automation, digital enablement of depot-level maintenance processes, immersive Augmented Reality/Virtual Reality training and aims to achieve fleet-wide implementation and sustainment. The Phase III effort will perform applied research and development, engineering integration, and technology transition tasks necessary to mature, deploy, and sustain advanced manufacturing and digital technologies across the FRC enterprise. Work will be performed in Johnstown, Pennsylvania (80%); and Patuxent River, Maryland (20%), and is expected to be completed in July 2031. Fiscal 2026 working capital (Defense) funds in the amount of $450,000; fiscal 2025 aircraft procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $9,910,000; and fiscal 2025 Next Generation and Research, Exploration, and Development funds in the amount of $600,000, will be obligated at the time of award. This contract was competed with one offer received. Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division, Lakehurst, New Jersey, is the contracting activity.

Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Melbourne, Florida, is awarded a $21,081,363 cost-plus-fixed-fee and firm-fixed-price order (N0001926F1043) against a previously issued basic ordering agreement (N0001925G0003). This order procures non-recurring engineering, including software development, modifications to the E-2D Avionics Flight Management Computer, systems engineering, integration, and test activities, to integrate the Second Generation Anti-jam Tactical Ultra High Frequency Radio (SATURN) for NATO capability into the E-2D Advanced Hawkeye aircraft. The order also procures the production and delivery of 94 SATURN modification kits. Work will be performed in Melbourne, Florida (99.1%); West Chester, Ohio (0.6%); Ronkonkoma, New York (0.2%); and Laval, Quebec, Canada (0.1%), and is expected to be completed in April 2028. Fiscal 2026 aircraft procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $21,081,363 will be obligated at the time of award. This order was not competed. Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

Green Build USA LLC, doing business as Federal Contracting Protocol,* Coatesville, Pennsylvania, is awarded a $10,877,052 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for the procurement of roofing sustainment, maintenance, and repair. Work will be performed at the Philadelphia Naval Business Center, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, and is expected to be completed by August 2031. Fiscal 2026 overhead (Navy) funds in the amount of $69,600 will be obligated at the time of award and will not expire at the end of the fiscal year. This contract was competitively procured via the System for Award Management website, with four offers received. The Naval Surface Warfare Center, Philadelphia Division, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, is the contracting activity (N64498-26-D-1020).

ARMY

CAPE-APTIM JV1 LLC,* Norcross, Georgia, was awarded a $200,000,000 cost-plus-fixed-fee, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for restoration of the Standard Chlorine of Delaware Superfund Site. Bids were solicited via the internet with three received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of July 26, 2031. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, is the contracting activity (W912BU-26-D-A011). (Awarded July 27, 2026).

ACC Construction Co. Inc., Augusta, Georgia, was awarded a firm-fixed-price contract for construction of a mission command center. The amount of this action is $87,580,808 with a total cumulative face value of $92,975,763. Bids were solicited via the internet with three received. Work will be performed in Fort Bragg, North Carolina, with an estimated completion date of July 12, 2029. Fiscal 2026 military construction, defense-wide funds in the amount of $87,580,808 were obligated at the time of the award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Wilmington, North Carolina, is the contracting activity (W912PM-26-C-A017).

Sikorsky Aircraft Corp., Stratford, Connecticut, was awarded a $56,789,419 modification (P00002) to undefinitized contract action W58RGZ-26-C-0008 for additional advanced procurement funding for UH-60M Black Hawk Army aircraft. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $121,789,419. Work will be performed in Stratford, Connecticut, with an estimated completion date of Dec. 31, 2027. Fiscal 2025 aircraft procurement, Army funds in the amount of $56,789,419 were obligated at the time of the award. Army Contracting Command, Redstone Arsenal, Alabama, is the contracting activity.

Tirghra JV,* LLC, San Antonio, Texas, was awarded a $42,500,000 firm-fixed-price contract for cybersecurity, systems engineering, and program protection support. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of July 26, 2030. Army Contracting Command, Detroit Arsenal, Michigan, is the contracting activity (W912CH-26-DA079). (Awarded July 27, 2026).

General Dynamics-OTS Inc., Lincoln, Nebraska, was awarded a $33,911,620 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for empty BLU-129/B low collateral bomb cases. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of July 26, 2029. Army Contracting Command, Rock Island, Illinois, is the contracting activity (W519TC-26-D-A026). (Awarded July 27, 2026).

The Dutra Group, San Rafael, California was awarded a $28,175,500 firm-fixed-price contract for Charleston Lower Harbor maintenance dredging project work. Bids were solicited via the internet with four received. Work will be performed in Charleston, South Carolina, with an estimated completion date of Sept. 29, 2027. Fiscal 2026 civil operation and maintenance, funds in the amount of $28,175,500 were obligated at the time of the award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Charleston, South Carolina, is the contracting activity (W912HP-26-C-A013).

DigiFlight Inc., Columbia, Maryland, was awarded a $20,754,958 modification (P00002) to contract W31P4Q-25-F-0159 for programmatic support services. Bids were solicited via the internet with four received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of April 29, 2027. Army Contracting Command, Redstone Arsenal, Alabama, is the contracting activity.

Lockheed Martin Sippican Inc., Liverpool, New York, was awarded a $19,791,630 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for production of Stinger missile warheads and fuzes. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of July 28, 2026. Army Contracting Command, Newark, New Jersey, is the contracting activity (W15QKN-26-F-A236).

Miller Electric Co. Inc., Reno, Nevada, was awarded a $15,000,000 firm-fixed-price contract to provide construction services for the maintenance, design, construction, or repair of real property facilities. Bids were solicited via the internet with three received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of July 31, 2031. Army 419th Contracting Support Brigade, Fort McCoy, Wisconsin, is the contracting activity (W911SA-26-D-A022). (Awarded July 27, 2026).

Altech Inc.,* Texarkana, Texas, was awarded a $13,000,000 firm-fixed-price contract for the modernization of buildings to support advanced manufacturing capabilities. Bids were solicited via the internet with three received. Work will be performed in Texarkana, Texas, with an estimated completion date of July 29, 2027. Fiscal 2026 aircraft procurement, Army funds in the amount of $13,000,000 were obligated at the time of the award. Army Contracting Command, Detroit Arsenal, Michigan, is the contracting activity (W911RQ-26-C-A015). (Awarded July 27, 2026).

HME Construction Inc.,* Vancouver, Washington, was awarded an $11,502,000 firm-fixed-price contract for clamshell maintenance dredging and associated hydrographic and land survey work. The amount of this action is $11,502,000 with a total cumulative face value of $12,999,900. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work will be performed in Portland, Oregon, with an estimated completion date of March 27, 2027. Fiscal 2026 civil operation and maintenance funds in the amount of $11,502,000 were obligated at the time of the award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Portland, Oregon, is the contracting activity (W9127N-26-C-A028).

DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY

Mainstream Engineering Corp.,* Rockledge, Florida, has been awarded a maximum $10,357,000 firm-fixed-price contract for aircraft carrier release elements. This was a sole-source acquisition using justification 10 U.S. Code 3204 (a)(1), as stated in Federal Acquisition Regulation 6.302-1. This is a two-year three-month contract with no option periods. The performance completion date is Oct. 5, 2028. Using military service is Navy. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2028 defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics agency Land and Maritime, Columbus, Ohio (SPE7M2-26-C-0012).

*Small business
