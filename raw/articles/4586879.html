ARMY

Action Manufacturing Co.,* Bristol, Pennsylvania, was awarded a $712,480,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract to manufacture, inspect, test, package and deliver M739A1 point detonating/delay fuzes. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 31, 2031. Army Contracting Command, Newark, New Jersey, is the contracting activity (W15QKN-26-D-A084).

South Carolina Commission for the Blind, Columbia, South Carolina, was awarded a $180,000,000 modification (P00002) to contract W9124C-25-D-A003 for full food services including all supervision, labor, personnel, equipment, materials and supplies other than government furnished property, to perform operational services of eleven Army dining facilities. The amount of this action is $180,000,000 with a total cumulative face value of $280,000,000. One bid was solicited with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of March 3, 2029. Army 419th Contracting Support Brigade, Fort Jackson, South Carolina, is the contracting activity. (Awarded Aug. 28, 2026).

AAECON General Contracting LLC,* Louisville, Kentucky (W912QR-26-D-A044); Amerifield LLC,* Berlin, Connecticut (W912QR-26-D-A045); Huot Construction and Services Inc.,* South Saint Paul, Minnesota (W912QR-26-D-A046); Red Eagle 3 JV,* Coweta, Oklahoma (W912QR-26-D-A047); Semper Tek Inc.,* Lexington, Kentucky (W912QR-26-D-A048); Valiant Construction LLC,* Louisville, Kentucky (W912QR-26-D-A049); and Tri Coast-Pac Tech JV 2,* Kelso, Washington (W912QR-26-D-A050), will compete for each order of the $160,000,000 firm-fixed-price contract for design-bid-build construction services. Bids were solicited via the internet with 19 received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Feb. 29, 2032. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Louisville, Kentucky, is the contracting activity.

Rockwell Collins Inc., Cedar Rapids, Iowa, was awarded a cost-plus-fixed-fee, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for command, control and communications integration, modification, maintenance, enhancement and engineering support for the Common Avionics Architecture System 10.3 software for the evolving fleet. The amount of this action is $38,916,896 with a total cumulative face value of $55,146,864. One bid was solicited with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Feb. 28, 2029. Army Contracting Command, Redstone Arsenal, Alabama, is the contracting activity (W58RGZ-26-F-A009).

Paligen Aerospace & Defense LLC,* Tampa, Florida, was awarded a $30,840,628 modification (P00005) to undefinitized contract action W519TC-25-F-0015 for design, construction and commissioning of a trinitrotoluene production facility. The total cumulative face value is $435,000,000. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Feb. 15, 2029. Army Contracting Command, Rock Island, Illinois, is the contracting activity.

CRB-PLS LLC, O'Brien, Florida, was awarded a $19,000,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for real estate title services. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 30, 2031. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Fort Worth, Texas, is the contracting activity (W9126G-26-D-A060).

KBR Services LLC, Houston, Texas, was awarded a $17,000,000 modification (001 CB) to contract W52P1J-12-G-0061 to complete care of supplies in storage and other maintenance of government equipment. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $165,655,369. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of June 1, 2027. Army Contracting Command, Rock Island, Illinois, is the contracting activity.

Tern AI Inc., Austin, Texas, was awarded an $11,263,793 firm-fixed-price contract for an independently derived positioning system and vehicle health and maintenance system to improve positioning, navigation and timing resilience and predictive sustainment capabilities. One bid was solicited with one received. Work will be performed in Austin, Texas, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 19, 2027. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation, Army funds in the amount of $11,263,793 were obligated at the time of award. Army FUZE Contracting Center, Arlington, Virginia, is the contracting activity (W51701-26-C-A205). (Awarded Aug. 28, 2026).

NAVY

AECOM Technical Services Inc., Los Angeles, California (N39430-26-D-2011); Aptim Federal Services LLC, Baton Rouge, Louisiana (N39430-26-D-2012); Argus Consulting Inc.,* Overland Park, Kansas (N39430-26-D-2013); Austin Brockenbrough & Associates LLC, Richmond, Virginia (N39430-26-D-2014); Burns & McDonnell Engineering Co. Inc., Kansas City, Missouri (N39430-26-D-2015); Enterprise Engineering Inc.,* Anchorage, Alaska (N39430-26-D-2016); HDR Engineering Inc., Omaha, Nebraska (N39430-26-D-2017); Pond & Co., Peachtree Corners, Georgia (N39430-26-D-2018); Robert and Co. Inc.,* Atlanta, Georgia (N39430-26-D-2019); and Tetra Tech Inc., Collinsville, Illinois (N39430-26-D-2020), were awarded a combined $145,000,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity, multiple award contract. This contract provides for petroleum, oil and lubricants engineering, and design services for systems and supporting facilities at various Navy and Marine Corps installations worldwide. Work is expected to be complete by Aug. 2031. The maximum dollar value for all 10 contracts is $145,000,000 over a five-year base period. Fiscal 2026 Navy working capital funds in the amount of $10,000 per awardee will be obligated at time of award to satisfy the minimum guarantee. This contract was competitively procured via the SAM.gov website, with 14 offers received. Naval Facilities Engineering and Expeditionary Warfare Center, Port Hueneme, California, is the contracting activity.

Environmental Chemical Corp., Burlingame, California, is awarded a $128,363,354 firm-fixed-price contract for construction services for the NATO Joint Force Command Norfolk's Facilities Phase II Interim Modular Facility. Work will be performed at Naval Support Activity, Norfolk, Virginia, and is expected to be completed by July 2028. The maximum dollar value, including the base price and 13 options, is $174,443,268. Fiscal 2026 NATO Security Investment Program funding in the amount of $128,363,354 will be obligated at the time of the award and will not expire at the end of the fiscal year. This contract was competitively procured via the SAM.gov website, with four offers received. Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command, Mid-Atlantic, Norfolk, Virginia, is the contracting activity (N40085-26-C-0020).

General Dynamics Information Technology Inc., Falls Church, Virginia, was awarded a $43,874,231 cost-only modification to previously awarded contract (N6339423C0009) for in-service engineering support of the MK 41 Vertical Launching System, including production of Module Electronic Box, Hazard Electronic Box, and Launch Control Panel Assemblies. Work will be performed in Falls Church, Virginia, and is expected to be completed by Aug. 2028. Fiscal 2026 presidential drawdown authority funds in the amount of $43,874,231 will be obligated at time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Naval Surface Warfare Center, Port Hueneme Division, Port Hueneme, California, is the contracting activity (Awarded Aug. 27, 2026).

Lockheed Martin Corp., Moorestown, New Jersey, is awarded $26,152,726 for a firm-fixed-price contract for the refurbishment of 44 5W83 VLA thrust vectors and 44 VH07 VLA rocket motors used on Navy cruisers and destroyers. The contract does not contain an option provision. All work under the contract will be performed in Moorestown, New Jersey, and is expected to be completed by May 2029. Fiscal 2026 working capital funds (Navy) in the amount of $24,845,090 (95%); and fiscal 2025 working capital funds (Navy) in the amount of $1,307,636 (5%), will be obligated at time of award and those funds will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. One company was solicited for this sole-source requirement pursuant to the authority set forth in 10 U.S. Code 3204 (a)(1) and one offer received. Naval Supply Systems Command Weapon Systems Support, Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania, is the contracting activity (N00104-26-C-K215).

KBR Wyle Services LLC, Lexington Park, Maryland, is awarded a $25,162,568 cost-plus-fixed-fee modification (P00003) to a previously awarded contract (N0042125C1001). The modification exercises an option to provide continued program management, engineering, financial, and logistics support for the F/A-18 air vehicle, subsystems, armament, engine, and airborne weapons for the Navy and Foreign Military Sales (FMS) customers. Work will be performed in Patuxent River, Maryland (78%); Kuwait (7%); Philadelphia, Pennsylvania (7%); New Orleans, Louisiana (4%); and North Island, California (4%), and is expected to be completed in Aug. 2027. FMS customer funds in the amount of $23,213,236 will be obligated at the time of the award, none of which will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was not competed. Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

Textron Systems Corp., Hunt Valley, Maryland, is awarded a $15,472,272 firm-fixed-price order (N0001926F1170) against previously issued basic ordering agreement (N0001926G1007). This order provides pre-operational and operational support, associated data, environmental shock testing, and familiarization training in support of sea-based unmanned aircraft systems maritime intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance services aboard two Navy vessels. Work will be performed at various undisclosed locations outside the continental U.S. and is expected to be completed in May 2028. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Navy) funds in the amount of $15,472,272 will be obligated at the time of award. This order was competed. Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

The Boeing Co., St. Louis, Missouri, is awarded a $12,680,717 modification (P00094) to a previously awarded a fixed-price-incentive-fee, cost-plus-incentive-fee, cost-plus-fixed-fee contract (N0001918C1012). This modification adds scope to provide non-recurring engineering and integration efforts for updated Radio Frequency Blanker Unit and Encrypted Mass Storage System components with an embedded Data Transfer System in support of the MQ-25 program. Additionally, this modification exercises an option to provide deficiency analysis and corrections in support of security assessments of the Mission Management System Computer. Work will be performed in St. Louis, Missouri (85%); and Fairborn, Ohio (15%), and is expected to be completed by November 2029. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation (Navy) funds in the amount of $12,680,717 will be obligated at the time of award. This contract was not competed. Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

Meggitt Defense Systems Inc., Irvine, California, is awarded a $9,373,421 firm-fixed-price job order for Liquid Air Palletized System Kits, non-recurring engineering tooling and air handling unit controller and fan spare parts for use in Navy P8-A aircraft in support of the Tactical Airborne Sensor Mission System. These items form the environmental conditioning system of the Navy P8-A aircraft which provides cooling to critical sensor systems. This contract includes options which, if exercised, would bring the cumulative value of this contract to $20,509,871. This contract action was a sole source action in accordance with 10 U.S. Code 3204(a)(1), only one responsible source and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements. Work will be performed in Irvine, California, and is expected to be completed by September 2028. If all options are exercised, work will continue through fiscal 2030. Fiscal 2025 aircraft Procurement Navy funds in the amount of $9,273,584 (98.94%); fiscal 2024 aircraft procurement Navy funds in the amount of $73,443 (0.78%); and fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $26,394 (0.28%), will be obligated at time of award, of which $26,394 will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Naval Surface Warfare Center, Crane Division, Crane, Indiana, is the contracting activity (N0016426FL049).

AIR FORCE

Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Riverdale, Utah, has been awarded an $86,069,000 task order (FA8214-26-F-B017) under previously awarded ground subsystem support contract 2.0 FA8214-21-D-0002 for remote visual assessment (RVA) program support. This task order provides for program management, maintenance, sustaining engineering, and support services for the RVA program. Work will be performed at Hill Air Force Base, Utah; F.E. Warren AFB, Wyoming; Malmstrom AFB, Montana; and Minot AFB, North Dakota, and is expected to be completed by September 2031. This award is the result of a sole-source acquisition. Fiscal 2026 procurement funds in the amount of $15,240,085 are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Nuclear Weapons Center, Hill AFB, Utah, is the contracting activity (FA8214-26-F-B017).

Aleut Construction LLC,* Reston, Virginia, has been awarded a $52,525,333 modification (P00001) to previously awarded contract FA8501-25-C-0005 for a narrow body paint booth. This modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $56,116,956 from $3,591,623. Work will be performed at Robins Air Force Base, Warner Robins, Georgia, and is expected to be completed by March 15, 2028. Fiscal 2026 funds in the amount of $52,525,333 are being obligated at the time of award. Air Force Materiel Command Operational Contracting, Robins AFB, Warner Robins, Georgia, is the contracting activity.

CAE Inc., Arlington, Texas, has been awarded a maximum $42,103,873 fixed-price, cost-plus-award-fee, cost reimbursable, and fixed-price plus incentive contract for Royal Moroccan Air Force F-16 Block 72 training system and contractor logistics support. The contract provides for two F-16 full mission trainer systems to include instructor-operator stations, F-16 Block 72 cockpit, visual display system, computational system, power distribution system, joint helmet-mounted cueing system ii helmet-mounted display, one uninterruptable power supply, one central file server, spares and support equipment.  Work will be performed in Arlington, Texas; and Sidi Slimane Airbase, Morocco, and is expected to be completed by April 30, 2029.  This contract involves Foreign Military Sales (FMS) to Morocco.  FMS funds in the amount of $37,975,064 are being obligated at the time of award.  Air Force Life Cycle Management Center, Agile Combat Support Directorate, Simulators Division, Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio, is the contracting activity (FA862126CB003).

U.S. SPECIAL OPERATIONS COMMAND

Raytheon Co., McKinney, Texas, is being awarded a $9,747,240 task order (H9240826FE010), under an indefinite delivery-indefinite quantity contract for the Silent Knight Radar Antennae Gimbal-4 program. Fiscal 2026 e procurement funds in the amount of $3,684,694; and fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $215,814, will be obligated at time of award for a total of $3,900,508. The work will be performed in McKinney, Texas, and is expected to be completed by Sept. 14, 2027. Contract funds will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. The contract (H9240824D4343) was awarded under authority 10 U.S. Code 3204(a)(1). U.S. Special Operations Command, MacDill Air Force Base, Florida, is the contracting activity.

DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY

Kit Masters Inc.,* Perham, Minnesota, has been awarded an estimated $9,235,200 firm-fixed-price contract for engine fan clutches. This was a sole-source acquisition using justification 10 U.S. Code 3204 (a)(1), as stated in Federal Acquisition Regulation 6.302-1. This is a two-year base contract with one one-year option period. The performance completion date is June 19, 2028. Using military service is Army. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2028 Army working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Warren, Michigan (SPRDL1-26-C-0111).

*Small business
