ARMY

Lockheed Martin Corp., Grand Prairie, Texas, was awarded a $53,859,843,289 firm-fixed-price modification (P00002) to contract W31P4Q-26-C-0013 for the PATRIOT Advanced Capability-3 (PAC-3) Production program, converting the current one-year undefinitized contract action into a seven-year multiyear procurement to increase the not-to-exceed ceiling and encompass all hardware, equipment and manufacturing efforts required to produce PAC-3 Missile Segment Enhancement missiles. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $58,620,843,289. Work will be performed in Huntsville, Alabama; Camden, Arkansas; Hollister, California; Clearwater, Lake Mary, Ocala, and Pinellas Park, Florida; Wichita, Kansas; Chelmsford, Massachusetts; Lincoln, Nebraska; East Aurora, New York; Archbald, Pennsylvania; Grand Prairie and Lufkin, Texas; Vergennes, Vermont; and Rocket Center, West Virginia, with an estimated completion date of March 31, 2035. No funds were obligated at the time of the award. Army Contracting Command, Redstone Arsenal, Alabama, is the contracting activity. (Awarded July 29, 2026).

CACI Inc. - Federal, Chantilly, Virginia, was awarded a $500,000,000 firm-fixed-price, single-award, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for the procurement of commercial items from a commercial catalog, including counter-unmanned aerial systems and counter-small-unmanned aerial systems capabilities. Bids were solicited via the internet with 50 received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of July 15, 2029. Army Contracting Command, Detroit Arsenal, Michigan, is the contracting activity (W912CH-26-D-A093). (Awarded July 28, 2026).

Sikorsky Aircraft Corp., Stratford, Connecticut, was awarded a $49,929,599 cost-plus-fixed-fee modification (P00027) to contract W58RGZ-22-C-0063 for procurement of services and data for the integration and qualification of the Improved Turbine Engine onto the UH/HH-60M Black Hawk. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $137,968,024. Work will be performed in Stratford, Connecticut, with an estimated completion date of June 30, 2030. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation, Army funds in the amount of $49,929,599 were obligated at the time of the award. Army Contracting Command, Redstone Arsenal, Alabama, is the contracting activity.

The Haskell Co., Jacksonville, Florida, was awarded a firm-fixed-price contract for hangar renovations. The amount of this action is $45,223,000 with a total cumulative face value of $65,183,659. Bids were solicited via the internet with 11 received. Work will be performed in Patrick Space Force Base, Florida, with an estimated completion date of April 11, 2029. Fiscal 2010 civil construction funds in the amount of $45,223,000 were obligated at the time of the award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Jacksonville, Florida, is the contracting activity (W912EP-26-C-A022).

Donjon Marine Co. LLC, Hillside, New Jersey, was awarded a $24,703,050 firm-fixed-price contract for maintenance dredging of portions of the New York and New Jersey Channels. Bids were solicited via the internet with four received. Work will be performed in Carteret, New Jersey, with an estimated completion date of Nov. 3, 2026. Fiscal 2026 civil operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $24,703,050 were obligated at the time of the award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, New York, New York, is the contracting activity (W912DS-26-C-A012).

Raytheon Co., Fullerton, California, was awarded a $25,000,000 cost-plus-fixed-fee, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for depot-level repair, engineering and obsolescence sustainment of the AN/USC-28(V) strategic communications terminal. One bid was solicited with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of July 30, 2031. Army Contracting Command, Aberdeen Proving Ground, Maryland, is the contracting activity (W15P7T-26-D-A004).

UPDATE: The $19,791,630 firm-fixed-price, indefinite delivery/indefinite-quantity, undefinitized contract action (W15QKN-26-F-A236) announced on July 28, 2026, to Lockheed Martin Sippican Inc., Liverpool, New York, to produce Stinger missile warheads and fuzes, was actually awarded on July 30, 2026.

AIR FORCE

Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Rolling Meadows, Illinois, has been awarded a $1,840,000,000 ceiling, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity, firm-fixed-price contract for Laser Infrared Targeting and Navigation Comprehensive Advanced Targeting Pod. This contract provides for continued sustainment, production, and modifications for the LITENING CATP. Work will be performed at Rolling Meadows, Illinois, and is expected to be completed by Dec. 31, 2035. This contract was a sole source acquisition. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $42,911,770 are being obligated at time of award. The Precision Attack Contracting Branch, Robins Air Force Base, Georgia, is the contracting activity (FA8540-26-D-B001).

Amentum Technology Inc., Tullahoma, Tennessee (FA255326DB001); BAE Systems Inc., Broomfield, Colorado (FA255326DB002); CACI Inc., Chantilly, Virginia (FA255326DB003); Firefly Aerospace, Leander, Texas (FA255326DB004); L3Harris Technologies Inc., Palm Bay, Florida (FA255326DB005); Lockheed Martin Space, Littleton, Colorado (FA255326DB006); Northrup Grumman System Corp., Dulles, Virginia (FA255326DB007); Pacific Crest Alliance, Colorado Springs, Colorado (FA255326DB008); Parsons Government Services Inc., Centreville, Virginia (FA255326DB009); Redwire Space Missions LLC, Littleton, Colorado (FA255326DB010); Rocket Lab USA Inc., Long Beach, California (FA255326DB011); Sierra Space Corp., Louisville, Colorado (FA255326DB012); The Boeing Co., Colorado Springs, Colorado (FA255326DB013); Viasat Inc., Carlsbad, California (FA255326DB014); and York Space Systems, Denver, Colorado (FA255326DB015), were awarded a ceiling $981,000,000 multiple award, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for National Space Test and Training Complex Innovative Technology and Engineering - Space Test and Range Capability Development. This contract provides for support to space and ground-based engineering activities to enhance test, evaluation, and training operations. Work will be performed at various locations and is expected to be completed by July 29, 2032. These contracts were competitive acquisitions, and twenty offers were received. No funds are being obligated at time of award. The Space Systems Command, Operational Test and Training Infrastructure, Colorado Springs, Colorado, is the contracting activity.

Decryptor Inc., Richardson, Texas (FA8250-26-D-B001); Design Criteria Inc., Roy, Utah (FA8250-26-D-B002); Diamond Antenna & Microwave Corp., Littleton, Massachusetts (FA8250-26-D-B003); DuoTech Services LLC, Franklin, North Carolina (FA8250-26-D-B004); Elbit America Inc., Fort Worth, Texas (FA8250-26-D-B005); Everaxis USA Inc., Naples, Florida (FA8250-26-D-B006); G&S Test Solutions LLC, Oviedo, Florida (FA8250-26-D-B007); Linmarr Associates Inc., Irvine, California (FA8250-26-D-B008); Potomac Electric Corp., Boston, Massachusetts (FA8250-26-D-B009); Serco Inc., Herndon, Virginia (FA8250-26-D-B010); Southwest Research Institute, San Antonio, Texas (FA8250-26-D-B011); Telecommunication Support Services Inc., West Melbourne, Florida (FA8250-26-D-B012); CPI Electron Device Business Inc., Beverly, Massachusetts (FA8250-26-D-B013); J-Squared Technologies (North Carolina) Inc., Berkeley Lake, Georgia (FA8250-26-D-B014); Kihomac Inc., Reston, Virginia (FA8250-26-D-B015); Pro-Comm Inc., Brick, New Jersey (FA8250-26-D-B016); and United States Technologies Inc., Fair Lawn, New Jersey (FA8250-26-D-B017), have been awarded ceiling $300,000,000 indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity, firm-fixed-price, multiple award contracts for repair services. This contract provides essential repair services for the 415th Supply Chain Management Squadron. Work will be performed at Richardson, Texas; Roy, Utah; Littleton, Massachusetts; Franklin, North Carolina; Fort Worth, Texas; Naples, Florida; Oviedo, Florida; Irvine, California; Boston, Massachusetts; Herndon, Virginia; San Antonio, Texas; West Melbourne, Florida; Beverly, Massachusetts; Berkeley Lake, Georgia; Reston, Virginia; Brick, New Jersey; and Fair Lawn, New Jersey; and is expected to be completed by July 29, 2036. These contracts were competitive acquisitions, and 25 offers were received. Fiscal 2026 working capital funds in the amount of $500 (per awardee) are being obligated at time of award. The Air Force Sustainment Center, Hill Air Force Base, Utah, is the contracting activity.

Northrop Grumman Aerospace Systems, San Diego, California, has been awarded a $17,747,040 cost-plus-fixed-fee, indefinite-delivery requirements contract modification (P00018) to previously awarded contract FA8539-21-D-0001 for a form, fit, function engineering change proposal of the digital receiver module of the RQ-4B Global Hawk Unmanned Air-Vehicle system. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $618,228,400. Work will be performed at San Diego, California; El Segundo, California; and Forest, Mississippi, and is expected to be completed by Nov. 30, 2028. Fiscal 2026 contract depot maintenance direct cite funds in the amount of $17,747,040 are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Sustainment Center, Robins Air Force Base, Georgia, is the contracting activity.

NAVY

RTX Corp., Pratt and Whitney Military Engines, East Hartford, Connecticut, is awarded a $1,295,745,898 undefinitized contract action modification (P00024) to a previously awarded firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract (N0001920D0013). This modification adds scope to procure initial spares for the F135 propulsion system, including depot and common spares for the F-35 global spares pool, and country unique spares in support of the Joint Strike Fighter program for the Air Force, Marine Corps, Navy, non-Department of Defense participants, F-35 Cooperative Program Partners, and Foreign Military Sales customers. Work will be performed in East Hartford, Connecticut (59.6%); Middletown, Connecticut (16.7%); Windsor, Connecticut (7.5%); North Berwick, Maine (5%); Red Oak, Texas (2.4%); Kent, Washington (1.6%); Wallingford, Connecticut (1.5%); Smithfield, Rhode Island (1.4%); El Cajon, California (1.1%); San Diego, California (0.9%); and various other locations within the continental U.S. (2.3%), and is expected to be completed in December 2030. No funds will be obligated at the time of award; funds will be obligated on individual orders as they are issued. The contract that is being modified was not competed. The Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

American Engineering Corp., Okinawa, Japan (N40084-26-D-1501); Fuji Construction Co. Ltd., Okinawa, Japan (N40084-26-D-1502); Kraftia-Yabu JV, Okinawa, Japan (N40084-26-D-1503); Kyunan Co. Ltd., Miyazaki, Japan (N40084-26-D-1504); Maeda-Onaka JV, Okinawa, Japan (N40084-26-D-1505); Nippo Corp., Tokyo, Japan (N40084-26-D-1506); Sanken-Witco JV, Tokyo, Japan (N40084-26-D-1507); Sumiju Yokosuka Kogyo Co. Ltd., Yokosuka, Japan (N40084-26-D-1508); and TODA-NUDP JV, Okinawa, Japan (N40084-26-D-1509), are awarded a combined $1,000,000,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity, design-build/design-bid-build, multiple award construction contract. This contract provides for project design as well as construction, renovation, modernization, repair, and maintenance projects. Work will be performed at U.S. government installations in the Okinawa, Japan, region and is expected to complete by July 2034. Kraftia-Yabu JV will be awarded $54,971,533 for the seed project. All remaining awardees will be awarded $6,647 each for the minimum contract guarantee at contract award. The maximum combined dollar value, including the base period of five years and one option period of three years, for all nine contracts is $1,000,000,000. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Marine Corps) in the amount of $55,024,709 will be obligated at time of award and will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was competitively procured via the SAM.gov website, with 14 offers received. Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command, Far East, Yokosuka, Japan, is the contracting activity.

Manson Construction Co. (N44255-26-D-1501), Seattle, Washington; DOD Marine Constructors JV (N44255-26-D-1502), Napa, California; Triton Marine Construction Corp. (N44255-26-D-1503), Bremerton, Washington; Michels Construction Inc. (N44255-26-D-1504), Brownsville, Wisconsin; Orion Government Services LLC (N44255-26-D-1505), Federal Way, Washington; Kiewit Infrastructure West Co. (N44225-26-D-1506), Vancouver, Washington; Contrack Watts Inc. (N44255-26-D-1507), McLean, Virginia; SLSCO Ltd. (N44255-26-D-1508), Galveston, Texas; and Ballard Marine Construction LLC (N44255-26-D-1509), Washougal, Washington, are awarded a combined-maximum-value $700,000,000 indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity, multiple-award construction contract for new construction, renovation, alteration, demolition, and repair work for marine piers and facilities at various locations or other similar facilities at various locations within the Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command Northwest area of operations, and will complete no later than July 2028. Nine awardees will be awarded $5,000 each at contract award. This contract was competitively procured via SAM.gov with 13 proposals received. Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command Northwest, Silverdale, Washington, is the contracting activity.

Orbis Sibro Inc., Charleston, South Carolina (N00189-26-D-L010); Mantech Advanced Systems International Inc., Herndon, Virginia (N00189-26-D-L011); QED Systems Inc., Virginia Beach, Virginia (N00189-26-D-L012); and HII Mission Technologies LLC, McLean, Virginia (N00189-26-D-L013); are being awarded $147,951,314 for a multiple-award, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract to provide Commander, Naval Air Forces (CNAF) with aircraft carrier readiness support that includes professional engineering assistance and hazardous waste and material handling to ensure the protection of personnel and the environment. These contracts are firm-fixed-price with a five-year base ordering period with an option for an additional six-month ordering period pursuant to Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 52.217-8. Work under the contract will only be performed in response to awarded task orders. The majority of work under the contract (95%) will be performed in government facilities, to include aircraft carriers, based on CNAF's need with exact locations unable to be determined at this time. Additional work will be performed in contractor facilities. Due to the multiple award nature of the contract that requires delivery order requirements to be competed among contract holders and that work be competed among the four contractors, a further breakdown of the work by geographic location at contractor facilities cannot be reasonably estimated at the time of contract award. The ordering period for the contract is anticipated to begin in August 2026 and continue through July 2031, and if the six-month option period is exercised the ordering period would continue through January 2032. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Navy) funds in each contract's minimum guarantee amount of $2,500 (for a total of $10,000 to cover the four contracts) will be obligated at the time of award and will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Subsequent task orders under the resultant contract will be funded with appropriate fiscal year operations and maintenance funds (Navy) or research, development, test and evaluation (Navy) funds and the expiration of those funds will depend on the funds used when the orders are issued. This contract was competitively procured through the System for Award Management website (Sam.gov) with six offers received and resulted from a full and open competitive solicitation that provided for the award of multiple contracts pursuant to FAR 16.504 and FAR Part 15 (Contracting by Negotiation). Naval Supply Systems Command Fleet Logistics Center Norfolk, Norfolk, Virginia, is the contracting activity.

General Electric (GE) Aerospace, East Hartford, Connecticut, is awarded a $117,852,737 firm-fixed-price indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract (N0001926D1004). This contract is for commercial depot-level services regarding standard rework of Navy and Marine Corps T700-GE-401 series engines, cold section modules and power turbine modules. Work will be performed in Winfield, Kansas (100%), and is expected to be completed in August 2028. No funds will be obligated at the time of award; funds will be obligated on individual orders as they are issued. The contract that is being modified was not competed. The Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

AOR International LLC,* Melbourne, Florida (N00189-26-D-L015); Blue Water Autonomy Inc.,* Lexington, Massachusetts (N00189-26-D-L016); Chance Technologies LLC,* Lafayette, Louisiana (N00189-26-D-L017); Link Technologies Inc.,* Germantown, Maryland (N00189-26-D-L018); Ocean Power Technologies Inc.,* Monroe Township, New Jersey (N00189-26-D-L019); and Saildrone Inc.,* Alameda, California ((N00189-26-D-L0120), are being awarded $40,000,000 for a multiple-award, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract to provide high-resolution ocean floor mapping services using long-endurance unmanned surface vehicles for the Naval Oceanographic Office (NAVOCEANO). These contracts are firm-fixed-price with a two-year base ordering period with no option periods. Work under the contract will only be performed in response to awarded task orders. All work will be performed in international waters of the Indian Ocean and Pacific Basins beyond U.S. territorial boundaries based on NAVOCEANO's needs. Due to the multiple award nature of the contract that requires task order requirements to be competed among contract holders and that work be competed among the six contractors, a further breakdown of the work by geographic location at contractor facilities cannot be reasonably estimated at the time of contract award. The ordering period for the contract is anticipated to begin in August 2026 and continue through July 2028. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Navy) funds in each contract's minimum guarantee amount of $10,000 will be obligated at the time of award (for a total of $60,000 to cover the six contracts) and will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Subsequent task orders under the resultant contract will be funded with appropriate fiscal year operations and maintenance funds (Navy), and the expiration of those funds will depend on the funds used when the orders are issued. This contract was competitively procured through the System for Award Management website (Sam.gov) with nine offers received and resulted from a small business set-aside competitive solicitation that provided for the award of multiple contracts pursuant to Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 16.504 and FAR Part 15, Contracting by Negotiation. Naval Supply Systems Command Fleet Logistics Center Norfolk, Norfolk, Virginia, is the contracting activity.

Laurel Technologies Partnership, Largo, Florida, is awarded a $32,031,186 firm-fixed-price modification to a previously awarded contract (N00024-24-C-5539) to exercise options for the procurement of additional AN/SPQ-9B radar systems in support of Arleigh Burke-class guided-missile destroyers (DDG 147-149). Work will be performed in Largo, Florida, and is expected to be completed by July 2028. Fiscal 2025 shipbuilding and conversion (Navy) funds in the amount of $32,031,186 will be obligated at time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity.

Lockheed Martin Corp. Rotary & Mission Systems, Moorestown, New Jersey, is being awarded a $29,425,505 cost-plus-fixed-fee order (N6278626F0032) to a previously awarded contract (N00024-18-C-2301) to provide engineering and management support for the lead ship Multi-Mission Surface Combatant (MMSC-1) post shakedown availability, an industrial post-delivery availability, and the continuous maintenance availability. This contract involves purchases for the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia under the foreign military sales program. Foreign Military Sales (Saudi Arabia) funding in the amount of $26,889,149 will be obligated at the time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. The Supervisor of Shipbuilding, Conversion, and Repair, Bath, Maine, is the contracting activity.

Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity (N00024-26-C-2409).

DEFENSE HEALTH AGENCY

A Square Group LLC,* Frederick, Maryland (HT003826DE002); Accenture Federal Services LLC, Arlington, Virginia (HT003826DE003); CACI Inc.-Federal, Chantilly, Virginia (HT003826DE004); Deep Mile Networks LLC, Vienna, Virginia (HT003826DE005); Deloitte Consulting LP, Arlington, Virginia (HT003826DE006); General Dynamics Information Technology Inc., Falls Church, Virginia (HT003826DE007); Great Hill Solutions LLC, Chantilly, Virginia (HT003826DE008); Kearney & Co. P.C., Alexandria, Virginia (HT003826DE009); Leidos Inc., Reston, Virginia (HT003826DE010); Peraton Inc., Herndon, Virginia (HT003826DE011); Planned Systems International Inc., Columbia, Maryland (HT003826DE012); and Vista Global Solutions LLC,* San Antonio, Texas (HT003826DE013), were awarded a combined $300,000,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity, multiple-award contract to provide all services and materials necessary to support the on-demand deployment of products within the Program Executive Office, Defense Healthcare Management Systems portfolio and related supporting components. The multiple-award contracts include a $10,000 minimum contract guarantee and a combined maximum value of $300,000,000 over a one-year base ordering period and four one-year option ordering periods. The ordering period is expected to run from August 2026 through August 2031. Individual task orders will establish the specific performance periods for each deployment activity. Services may be performed at locations within the continental U.S. and outside the continental U.S., supporting multiple roles of care and facility types. Specific work locations will be identified at the task order level. Funding will be obligated at the task order level as individual awards are issued. This contract was competitively procured through the System for Award Management (SAM.gov), with 29 complete offers received. The Defense Health Agency Contracting Office, Arlington, Virginia, is the contracting activity.

DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY

Labatt Institutional Supply Co., doing business as Labatt Food Service, San Antonio, Texas, has been awarded a maximum $69,928,930 firm-fixed-price with economic-price-adjustment, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for full-line food and beverage items.
