AIR FORCE

The Boeing Co., St. Louis, Missouri, has been awarded a $131,230,000,000 ceiling, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for the F-15 Eagle Crest in support of the F-15 Program Office. This contract provides for aircraft production, systems integration, modernization, upgrades, retrofits, sustainment, and the establishment of organic depot maintenance capabilities to deliver and maintain critical F-15 Eagle Weapon System capabilities for the Air Force, Air National Guard, and other Department of War customers. Work will be performed at St. Louis, Missouri, and is expected to be completed by August 2037. The ordering period is expected to end Aug. 24, 2031, with an option to extend the ordering period to Aug. 24, 2036. This contract involves Foreign Military Sales to Japan, Israel, Saudi Arabia, South Korea, Singapore, Indonesia, and Poland. This contract was a sole-source acquisition. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $343,740 are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Life Cycle Management Center, Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio, is the contracting activity (FA8634-26-D-B001).

University of New Mexico, Albuquerque, New Mexico, has been awarded a $27,408,016 cost-no-fee contract for Nuclear Systems Development, Nuclear Systems Integration, Nuclear System Flight Testing and Evaluation, and Nuclear Command, Control, and Communication. Work will be performed in Albuquerque, New Mexico, and is expected to be completed by Nov. 24, 2031. This contract was a competitive acquisition, and one offer was received. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $344,167 are being obligated at time of award. The Air Force Research Laboratory, Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico, is the contracting activity (FA9453-26-C-X021).

ARMY

Hardwire LLC,* Pocomoke City, Maryland, was awarded a $99,000,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for work on advanced processing of metallic armor under Small Business Innovative Research Phase III. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 23, 2031. Army Contracting Command, Aberdeen Proving Ground, Maryland, is the contracting activity (W911QX-26-D-A006).

Great Lakes Dredge & Dock Co. LLC, Houston, Texas, was awarded a $68,869,200 firm-fixed-price contract for improvement dredging associated with the New Haven Harbor Federal Navigation Project. Bids were solicited via the internet with four received. Work will be performed in New Haven, Connecticut, with an estimated completion date of March 31, 2028. Fiscal 2026 civil construction funds, and fiscal 2026 direct funds in the amount of $68,869,200 were obligated at the time of the award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Concord, Massachusetts, is the contracting activity (W912WJ-26-C-A017). (Awarded Aug. 20, 2026).

General Dynamics Land Systems Inc., Sterling Heights, Michigan, was awarded a $54,281,616 cost-plus-fixed-fee, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for Stryker original equipment manufacturer sustainment services support, limited to field-level maintenance, field service representative support, and fielding activities when interactive electronic technical manuals and logistics products are not yet fully integrated. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 24, 2031. Army Contracting Command, Detroit Arsenal, Michigan, is the contracting activity (W912CH-26-D-0077).

Widman Inc.,* Godfrey, Illinois, was awarded a $30,000,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for property maintenance and repairs, including paving and sealing, chip and seal work, riprap and stone placement, and grading and earthwork for wetlands, floodplains and levees. Bids were solicited via the internet with three received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 23, 2031. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, St. Louis, Missouri, is the contracting activity (W912P9-26-D-A033).

Agate Construction Co. Inc.,* Egg Harbor Township, New Jersey, was awarded a $19,695,000 firm-fixed-price contract for repair or extension of existing storm water outfalls along the Atlantic Ocean coastline of Absecon Island. Bids were solicited via the internet with three received. Work will be performed in Atlantic City, New Jersey, with an estimated completion date of May 2, 2028. Fiscal 2025 civil construction, and fiscal 2025 rivers and harbors contributed and advanced funds in the amount of $19,695,000 were obligated at the time of the award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, is the contracting activity (W912BU-26-C-A037).

Navy

Saab Inc., East Syracuse, New York, is awarded a $74,636,753 modification (P00023) to a previously awarded firm-fixed-price contract (N0001923C0049). This modification procures five SPN-50 low rate initial production units. Work will be performed in Syracuse, New York (58.9%); and Gothenburg, Sweden (41.1%), and is expected to be completed in May 2029. Fiscal 2026 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $14,961,358; and fiscal 2026 Shipbuilding and Conversion (Navy) funds in the amount of $13,947,430, will be obligated at the time of award, none of which will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was not competed. Naval Air Systems Command Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace, Kongsberg, Norway, is awarded a $44,267,020 firm-fixed-price modification to the previously awarded contract action (M67854-25-C-0023) for Amphibious Combat Vehicle Medium Caliber Cannon PROTECTOR Remote Turret 20, hereafter referred to as the “Turret.” The total value of the contract if all options are exercised is $335,904,707. This contract modification supports the procurement of additional Initial Issue Provisioning Turret Spares, associated engineering change proposal (ECP) and repair and sustainment processes, and the obligation of funds for future ECPs and repair/sustainment efforts. Work will be performed in Johnstown, Pennsylvania (62%); and Kongsberg, Norway (38%), with an expected completion date of Aug. 27, 2030. Fiscal 2025 procurement (Marine Corps) reconciliation funds in the amount of $44,267,020 will be obligated at the time of award and will not expire at the end of the fiscal year. Portfolio Acquisition Executive Marine Corps, Quantico, Virginia, is the contracting activity (M67854-25-C-0023).

Triumph Gear Systems Inc., Park City, Utah, is being awarded $20,932,317 for a not-to-exceed delivery order (N00383-26-F-S803) under a previously awarded basic ordering agreement (N00383-22-G-S801) for the repair of 63 mechanical actuators supporting Navy V-22 Osprey aircraft. The delivery order does not include an option provision. All work is expected to be completed by December 2027 and will be performed in Park City, Utah. Fiscal 2026 working capital funds (Navy) in the amount of $15,699,237 (75%) will be obligated at the time of award and those funds will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. An additional $5,233,079 remains committed to fund the not-to-exceed delivery order that has a maximum total value of $20,932,317. One source was solicited for this sole-source requirement pursuant to the authority set forth in 10 U.S. Code 3204 (a)(1) and one offer was received. Naval Supply Systems Command Weapon Systems Support, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, is the contracting activity.

Textron Systems Corp., Hunt Valley, Maryland, is awarded a $15,546,143 firm-fixed-price order (N0001926F1157) against previously issued basic ordering agreement (N0001926G1007). This order provides for pre-operational support, operational support, post-operational support, and associated data in support of sea-based unmanned aircraft systems maritime intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance services on board two Navy vessels under maritime b-kit one and maritime b-kit two. Operational support includes 175 sensor data hours per month for each vessel. Additionally, this action procures associated environmental shock testing and familiarization training in support of the Navy. Work will be performed in various undisclosed locations outside of the continental U.S. (specific locations are classified) and is expected to be completed in March 2028. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Navy) funds in the amount of $15,546,143 will be obligated at the time of award; all of which will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract is being awarded following a limited sources competition. Competition was limited to four vendors. Offers were received from all four contractors. Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY

Avox System Inc., Lancaster, New York, has been awarded a maximum $37,583,889 firm-fixed-price contract for demand oxygen regulators. This was a sole-source acquisition using justification 10 U.S. Code 3204 (a)(1), as stated in the Federal Acquisition Regulation 6.103-1. This is a five-year contract with no option periods. The performance completion date is Aug. 23, 2031. Using military service is Air Force. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2031 defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Richmond, Virginia (SPE4A7-26-D-0417).

*Small business
