ARMY

Hydrogeologic Inc.,* Reston, Virginia (W9128F-26-D-A032); EA Engineering, Science and Technology Inc. PBC,* Hunt Valley, Maryland (W9128F-26-D-A026); North Wind Site Services LLC,* Idaho Falls, Idaho (W9128F-26-D-A027); TUI America JV LLC,* Elkridge, Maryland (W9128F-26-D-A023); Dawson Solutions LLC,* Huntsville, Alabama (W9128F-26-D-A030); AEC-Weston JV LLC,* Knoxville, Tennessee (W9126F-26-D-A031); Cape Environmental Management Inc.,* Norcross, Georgia (W9128F-26-D-A022); Bay West LLC,* St. Paul, Minnesota (W9128F-26-C-A024); Bhate TLI JV LLC,* Birmingham, Alabama (W9128F-26-D-A025); Kemron/Arrowhead JV LLC,* Atlanta, Georgia (W9128F-26-D-A028); and Noreas-PIKA-ICS JV,* Irvine, California (W9128F-26-D-A029), will compete for each order of the $249,000,000 firm-fixed-price contract for environmental remediation services. Bids were solicited via the internet with 24 received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of July 9, 2031. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Omaha, Nebraska, is the contracting activity.

Janus Research Group LLC, Evans, Georgia, was awarded a $200,000,000 firm-fixed-price contract for innovation facilitation and research support services. The total cumulative face value of the contract is $240,000,000. One bid was solicited with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of July 9, 2033. Army Field Directorate Office, Fort Eustis, Virginia, is the contracting activity (W911S0-26-D-A007).

Akima Facilities Operations LLC, Herndon, Virginia, was awarded a $36,685,831 modification (P00147) to contract W52P1J-20-F-0137, for maintenance, transportation and supply services. One bid was solicited with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Jan. 9, 2028. Army Contracting Command, Rock Island Arsenal, Illinois, is the contracting activity.

SAT-Lite Technologies LLC,* White Oak, Texas, was awarded a $9,900,000 firm-fixed-price contract for commercial off-the-shelf SAT-LTE hardware, including motorized satellite antennas and associated electronics. One bid was solicited with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of July 9, 2031. Army Contracting Command, Aberdeen Proving Ground, Maryland, is the contracting activity (W911SR-26-D-SATL).

AIR FORCE

Pivotal Space Inc., doing business as LEAP, Broomfield, Colorado, has been awarded a $93,000,000 Small Business Innovation Research Phase III indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract modification (P00006) to previously awarded (FA8821-25-D-B001) for performance evaluation for resupply, integration, and global expeditionary enablement. This modification brings the total cumulative face value to $100,000,000 from $7,000,000. Work will be performed in Broomfield, Colorado, and is expected to be completed by August 29, 2030. No funds are being obligated at time of award. The Space Systems Command, Peterson Space Force Base, Colorado, is the contracting activity.

Booz Allen Hamilton Inc., McLean, Virginia, was awarded a maximum $25,342,035 cost-plus-fixed-fee contract for quantum-accelerated technology advancement for national advantage. This contract provides for research, development, test and evaluation of quantum information science technology. Work will be performed at Mclean, Virginia, and is expected to be completed by July 9, 2031. This contract was a competitive acquisition, and two offers were received. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $55,000 are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Research Laboratory, Rome, New York, is the contracting activity (FA8750-26-C-B045).

Raytheon Co., Sterling, Virginia, has been awarded a $10,921,796 modification (P00005) to a previously awarded contract (FA4890-22-C-0018) to exercise Option Year Four for Mission Video Distribution System services support. This modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $53,441,223 from $42,519,428. Work will be performed at Shaw Air Force Base, South Carolina, and is expected to be completed by July 31, 2027. Fiscal 2026 operation and maintenance funds in the amount of $1,903,633 are being obligated at time of award. Headquarters Air Combat Command Acquisition Management and Integration Center, Langley Air Force Base, Virginia, is the contracting activity.

Continuity Global Solutions LLC, St. Joe, Florida, has been awarded a ceiling $9,999,999 indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for information protection services. This contract provides information protection services for building construction. Work will be performed at Misawa Air Base, Japan, and is expected to be completed by Aug. 31, 2031. This contract was a competitive acquisition, and five offers were received. No funds are being obligated at time of award. The 35th Contracting Squadron, Misawa Air Base, Japan, is the contracting activity (FA5205-26-D-0007).

ENSCO Inc., Vienna, Virginia, has been awarded a ceiling of $9,900,000 for regional phased ratio enhancement. This contract provides for enhanced global seismic detection capabilities. Work will be performed at Patrick Space Force Base, Florida, and is expected to be completed by July 9, 2031. This contract was a competitive acquisition, and one offer was received. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $375,000 are being obligated at time of award. The Acquisition Management and Integration Center, Detachment 2, Patrick AFB, Florida, is the contracting activity (FA70222-6-D-0005).

DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY

Pomp's Tire Service, New Berlin, Wisconsin, has been awarded an estimated $70,184,484 firm-fixed-price indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for wheel and tire assemblies for the Palletized Loading system. This was a competitive acquisition with one response received. This is a five-year contract with no option periods. The ordering period end date is July 10, 2031. Using military service is Army. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2031 Army working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Warren, Michigan (SPRDL1-26-D-0045).

Industries of the Blind Inc.,* Greensboro, North Carolina, has been awarded an estimated $17,320,697 modification (P00018) exercising the second one-year option period of a two-year base contract (SPE1C1-23-D-0059) with five one-year option periods for warehousing, storage, logistics, and distribution functions. This is a firm-fixed-price with economic-price-adjustment, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract. The ordering period end date is July 10, 2027. Using military services are Army, Navy, Air Force, and Marine Corps. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2027 defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Troop Support, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

NAVY

Bluestone Environmental Group Inc., Malvern, Pennsylvania, is awarded a $45,000,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for multi-media environmental compliance services for projects throughout the Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command (NAVFAC) Washington area of responsibility. Work will be performed as specified in individual task orders at Navy, Marine Corps, and federal facilities within the NAVFAC Washington area of responsibility, including Washington, D.C., Maryland, Virginia, and U.S. territories, with an expected completion date of January 2032. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Navy) funds in the amount of $50,000 will be obligated at the time of award to satisfy the minimum guarantee and will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was competitively procured via the SAM.gov website, with five offers received. Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command Washington, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity (N40080-26-D-1016).

L3 Technologies Inc., Chesapeake Sciences Division, Millersville, Maryland, is awarded a $42,677,977 indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract containing cost-plus-fixed-fee, firm-fixed-price, and cost only provisions. This contract is for hardware and services required to support towed array programs using towed array integrated product team telemetry. Work will be performed in Millersville, Maryland, and the ordering period will be completed in July 2031. Service cost center funds in the amount of $171,010 will be obligated at the first delivery order to satisfy the minimum guarantee and will not expire at the end of the fiscal year. This contract was not competitively procured under the authority of 10 U.S. Code 2304(c)(5) as implemented by Federal Acquisition Regulation 6.302-5: authorized or required by Statute and authorized by 15 U.S. Code 638(r)(1). Naval Undersea Warfare Center, Division Newport, Newport, Rhode Island, is the contracting activity (N66604-26-D-P600).

Northrop Grumman Systems Corp., Mission Systems Sector, Linthicum Heights, Maryland, is being awarded a $39,400,521 cost-plus-fixed-fee modification to a previously awarded contract (N00024-22-C-5520) to exercise an option for design agent engineering support of Surface Electronic Warfare Improvement Program Block Three systems. Work will be performed in Baltimore, Maryland, and is expected to be complete by August 2027. Fiscal 2026 shipbuilding and construction (Navy) funds in the amount of $6,000,000 will be obligated at time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity.

*Small business
