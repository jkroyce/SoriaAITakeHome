AIR FORCE

Symetrics Industries LLC, doing business as Extant Aerospace, Melbourne, Florida, has been awarded a maximum $147,310,777 indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for the ALE-47 countermeasure dispenser system. This contract provides for ALE-47 countermeasure dispenser system line replaceable unit and shop replaceable unit production. Work will be performed at Melbourne, Florida, and is expected to be completed by Aug. 27, 2032. This contract involves Foreign Military Sales to be cited on each individual order. This contract was a competitive acquisition, and two offers were received. Fiscal 2026 defense working capital funds in the amount of $1,104,613; fiscal 2025 aircraft procurement, Navy funds in the amount of $1,813,528; and fiscal 2026 aircraft procurement, Navy funds in the amount of $2,200,686, are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Life Cycle Management Center, Robins Air Force Base, Georgia, is the contracting activity (FA8523-26-D-0001).

Telos Corp., Ashburn, Virginia has been awarded a $37,777,500 indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract to support the Air Combat Command, intelligence community, risk management, and cybersecurity. This contract provides for modernization to the cybersecurity framework in implementing new and innovative Artificial Intelligence tools/capabilities. Work will be performed in San Antonio, Texas, and is expected to be completed by Jan. 31, 2031. This contract was a competitive and 30 offers were received. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $3,137,460 are being obligated at time of the award. The Air Combat Command, Acquisition Management and Integration Center, Joint Base San Antonio-Lackland, Texas, is the contracting activity (FA7037-26-D-0003).

Kwaan Tech LLC, Chantilly, Virginia, has been awarded a $29,999,937 firm-fixed-price contract for E-3 contract aircrew training and courseware development services. This contract provides for technical and program management requirements for the full spectrum of Air Force directed E-3 training. Work will be performed at Tinker Air Force Base, Oklahoma, and is expected to be completed by Aug. 31, 2029. This contract was a sole source acquisition. Fiscal 2027 operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $75,434 are being obligated at time of award. The Headquarters Air Combat Command Acquisition Management and Integration Center, Hampton, Virginia, is the contracting activity (FA489026C0012). (Awarded on Aug. 27, 2026).

BK Manufacturing Co. Inc.,* Arab, Alabama, has been awarded a maximum $28,000,000 firm-fixed-price, requirements, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for wings and fins. This contract provides for wings and fins for the AIM-120X and CATM-120X series of Advanced Medium-Range Air-to-Air Missiles. Work will be performed at Arab, Alabama, and is expected to be completed by Aug. 27, 2031. The contract was a sole source acquisition. No funds are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Life Cycle Management Center, Robins Air Force Base, Georgia, is the contracting activity (FA8556-26-D-B001).

Oneida Engineering Solutions LLC, Milwaukee, Wisconsin has been awarded a $20,239,787 firm-fixed-price contract for a Cost Benefit and Technical Analysis to Select Longer-Term Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) Response Action for private off-base drinking water wells at Eastern Region Air Force Installations. This contract provides for all labor, equipment, and materials necessary for the installation, operation, and maintenance of point-of-use treatment systems, along with associated PFAS sampling, laboratory analysis, data validation, and reporting. Work will be performed at Air Force Research Laboratory Rome, New York; Arnold Air Force Base, Tennessee; Avon Park Air Force Range, Florida; Cape Canaveral Space Force Station, Florida; Columbus AFB, Mississippi; Dover AFB, Delaware; Eglin AFB, Florida; Hanscom AFB, Massachusetts; Homestead Air Reserve Base, Florida; Hurlburt Field, Florida; Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling, Washington D.C.; Joint Base Andrews, Maryland; Joint Base Cape Cod, Massachusetts; Joint Base Charleston, South Carolina; Joint Base Langley-Eustis, Virginia; Joint Base McGuire-Dix-Lakehurst, New Jersey; Keesler AFB, Mississippi; MacDill AFB, Florida; Maxwell AFB, Alabama; Moody AFB, Georgia; New Boston Space Force Station, Massachusetts; Niagara Falls Air Reserve Station, New York; Patrick SFB, Florida; Pittsburgh Air Reserve Station/Air National Guard Base, Pennsylvania; Robins AFB, Florida; Seymour Johnson AFB, North Carolina; Shaw AFB, South Carolina; Tyndall AFB, Florida; and Westover Air Reserve Base, Massachusetts, and is expected to be completed by Aug. 27, 2031. This contract was a sole source acquisition. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $3,337,424 are being obligated at the time of award. The 772d Enterprise Sourcing Squadron, Joint Base San Antonio Lackland, Texas, is the contracting activity (FA8903-26-C-0007).

CORRECTION: The $31,291,904 contract awarded to Lockheed Martin Corp., Fort Worth, Texas (FA860426DB008), was awarded on Aug. 25, 2026, not Aug. 17, 2026.

NAVY

BAE Systems Land & Armaments L.P., Maple Grove, Minnesota, is awarded a $167,496,594 cost-plus-fixed-fee and cost modification action to previously awarded contract N00024-21-C-5393 for MK 41 Vertical Launching System Mechanical Design Agent support. This action includes options which, if exercised, would bring the cumulative value of this action to $343,300,000. This action combines purchases for the Navy (76%); and the government of Japan (24%), under the Foreign Military Sales (FMS) program. Work will be performed in Minneapolis, Minnesota (42%); Norfolk Virginia (18%); San Diego, California (18%); Aberdeen, South Dakota (9%); Bath, Maine (6%); Pascagoula, Mississippi (6%); and Redzikowo, Poland (1%), and is expected to be completed by July 2027. If all options are exercised, work will continue through July 2028. Fiscal 2023 shipbuilding and conversion (Navy) funds in the amount of $1,700,000 (38%); fiscal 2026 Defense-wide operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $1,114,519 (24%); FMS Japan funds in the amount of $1,107,908 (24%); and fiscal 2026 Defense-wide research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $654,404 (14%), will be obligated at time of award, of which $1,114,519 will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. The Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington Navy Yard, D.C., is the contracting activity.

Huntington Ingalls Inc., Ingalls Shipbuilding Division, Pascagoula, Mississippi, was awarded a $12,983,399 cost-plus-fixed-fee, with a special performance incentive, modification (A00402) to a previously awarded contract (N00024-21-C-4205) to purchase commercial material for Planning Yard Support for Procurement of Common Rail Upgrade for LPD-17 Class USS New Orleans (LPD 18). This contract announcement is one of three purchases for three different contract modifications, representing three separate requirements. Fiscal 2026 other procurements Navy funds in the amount of $12,983,399 were obligated at time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. The Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity. (Awarded Aug. 26, 2026).

ARMY

Resilient Energy and Infrastructure LLC,* Madison, Tennessee, was awarded a $49,060,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract under the Small Business Innovation Research Phase III program to procure rapid deployment hybrid microgrid systems and modular energy storage systems that provide autonomous, cybersecure and resilient power for expeditionary operations. One bid was solicited with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 27, 2031. Army Contracting Command, Aberdeen Proving Ground, Maryland, is the contracting activity (W911QX-26-D-A011).

Emerson Construction Co., Temple, Texas, was awarded a firm-fixed-price contract for the construction and installation of a chilled water central energy plant. The amount of this action is $27,794,900 with a total cumulative face value of $34,000,000. Bids were solicited via the internet with three received. Work will be performed in Fort Hood, Texas, with an estimated completion date of Oct. 9, 2028. Fiscal 2026 military construction, Army funds in the amount of $27,794,900 were obligated at the time of award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Fort Worth, Texas, is the contracting activity (W9126G-26-C-A032).

Intellistore Inc.,* Troy, Michigan, was awarded a firm-fixed-price contract to modernize automated storage and retrieval systems. The amount of this action is $18,712,603 with a total cumulative face value of $20,261,512. Bids were solicited via the internet with six received. Work will be performed in Anniston, Alabama, with an estimated completion date of Nov. 30, 2027. Fiscal 2026 capital investment funds in the amount of $18,712,603 were obligated at the time of award. Army Contracting Command, Detroit Arsenal, Michigan, is the contracting activity (W911KF-26-C-A040).

K2 Group Inc., McLean, Virginia, was awarded a $17,864,236 cost-no-fee, firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract to provide higher headquarters assessments as required by regulation and statute. Bids were solicited via the internet with three received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 27, 2031. Army Field Directorate Office, Fort Sam Houston, Texas, is the contracting activity (W9124J-26-F-A069).

Taumat LLC,* Silver Spring, Maryland, was awarded a $10,000,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract under the Small Business Innovation Research Phase III program to mature advanced thermal energy storage technologies through research, development, test and evaluation of thermal management materials, advanced coatings and related thermal mitigation technologies. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 30, 2031. Army Contracting Command, Aberdeen Proving Ground, Maryland, is the contracting activity (W911QX-26-D-A012).

Calibre Systems Inc., Alexandria, Virginia, was awarded a $9,740,964 cost-plus-fixed-fee contract for personnel, equipment, tools, materials, supervision, and other items and non-personal services necessary to provide technical support services. Fiscal 2010 operations and maintenance, Army funds in the amount of $9,740,964 were obligated at the time of award. Work will be performed in Alexandria, Virginia, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 31, 2027. Army Contracting Command, Aberdeen Proving Ground, Maryland, is the contracting activity (W91CRB-26-C-A038).

DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY

General Electric Co., Lynn, Massachusetts, has been awarded a maximum $319,548,644 modification (P00045) exercising the first two-year four-month option period of a six-year three-month base contract (SPE4AX-20-D-9445) with one two-year four-month option period and one one-year and five-month option period. This is a firm-fixed-price, requirements contract for J85 engine supplies. The performance completion date is Dec. 31, 2028. Using military services are Air Force and Navy. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2029 defense appropriated funds and defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Richmond, Virginia.

U.S. SPECIAL OPERATIONS COMMAND

MCP Computer Products Inc., San Marcos, California, is being awarded a $17,643,430 order (H9240326FE159) for Dell laptops and associated equipment. This is a firm-fixed-price order for Dell laptops and equipment from the General Services Administration blanket purchase agreement (47QTCA22A000C). Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance funds are being obligated at time of award. The primary performance location is U.S. Special Operations Command, MacDill Air Force Base, Florida . The contracting authority is USSOCOM, MacDill AFB, Florida.

DEFENSE ADVANCED RESEARCH PROJECTS AGENCY

Guidehouse Inc., McLean, Virginia, was awarded a $11,793,452 cost-plus-fixed-fee contract for advisory and assistance services, and systems engineering and technical assistance support. This contract provides expertise and experience to accomplish a broad range of administrative, technical, and analytical services to assist in performing the office mission for existing and planned Multi X Office programs. Work will be performed in Arlington, Virginia, with an expected completion date of October 2027. This contract was a competitive acquisition under solicitation number HR001126RE001, with one offer received. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $2,077,246 are obligated at the time of award. The Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency, Arlington, Virginia, is the contracting activity (HR001126FE052).

*Small business
** Woman-owned small business
