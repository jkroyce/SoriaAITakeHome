AIR FORCE

L. Wallace Construction Co. Inc., Blanchard, Oklahoma (FA8903-26-D-0012); Pure Sky Project Solutions LLC, Austin, Texas (FA8903-26-D-0017); Vision Construction International LLC, Fairbanks, Alaska (FA8903-26-D-0019); Topside Contracting LLC, San Antonio, Texas (FA8903-26-D-0020); Hasen Global LLC, New Braunfels, Texas (FA8903-26-D-0021); AR6 Construction & Engineering LLC, New Braunfels, Texas (FA8903-26-D-0022); TATCO RC JV LLC, Caputa, South Dakota (FA8903-26-D-0028); Willkompany Inc., National City, California (FA8903-26-D-0029); MH Contracting LLC, Helotes, Texas (FA8903-26-D-0052); B.C. Schmidt Construction Inc., Colusa, California (FA8903-26-D-0031); SBS-RSSI JV, Essex, Maryland (FA8903-26-D-0053); Maycor LLC, Pensacola, Florida (FA8903-26-D-0032); AOD & RBT JV, Texarkana, Texas (FA8903-26-D-0033); DCR Services & Construction Inc., Detroit, Michigan (FA8903-26-D-0036); Tri Coast-Pac Tech JV 2, Kelso, Washington (FA8903-26-D-0054); and Carroll's Roofing & Construction LLC, Arlington, Tennessee (FA8903-26-D-0042), were awarded a $450,000,000 ceiling, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity, firm-fixed-price, multiple-award task order contracts for roofing, repair, and replacement. This contract provides for low-slope, steep-slope, and metal roofing system work. Work will be performed at Department of the Air Force installations within the continental U.S., including Alaska, and is expected to be completed July 31, 2031.These contracts were competitive small business set-aside acquisitions, and 40 offers were received. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $16,000 ($1,000 per awardee) are being obligated at the time of award. The 772nd Enterprise Sourcing Squadron, Joint Base San Antonio–Lackland, Texas, is the contracting activity. (Awarded June 29, 2026).

Peraton Inc., Herndon, Virginia, has been awarded a $109,093,756 hybrid fixed-price, level-of-effort, and cost-plus-fixed-fee contract for the Space Weather Analysis and Forecast System. This contract provides for sustainment, maintenance, legacy capability migration, and the continued development and incorporation of enhancements to operations and cybersecurity. Work will be performed at Colorado Springs, Colorado, and is expected to be completed by February 18, 2033. This contract was a sole source acquisition. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $3,781,592; and fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $1,135,523, are being obligated at time of award. Space Systems Command Directorate of Contracting, Colorado Springs, Colorado, is the contracting activity (FA8815-26-C-B001).

K2 Space, Torrance, California, was awarded a $22,947,034 firm-fixed-price contract for Enterprise Space Terminals (EST). This contract provides for the procuring, integrating, launching, and operating of two ESTs on two separate K2 space buses as hosted payloads. Work will be performed at Torrance, California, and is expected to be completed by November 2028. This contract was leveraged using the FreeSol Broad Agency Announcement. Fiscal 2025 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $22,947,034 are obligated at the time of award. The Space Based Sensing and Targeting Directorate, Space Systems Command, Los Angeles Air Force Base, California, is the contracting activity (FA8819-26-C-B009).

Nanometrics Inc., Natka, Canada, has been awarded a $15,000,000 ceiling, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity, firm-fixed-price contract with for Geophysical Field System technical refresh. This contract provides hardware to replace Geophysical Field Systems seismometers and data acquisition subsystem equipment. The work will be performed at Natka, Canada, and is expected to be completed July 31, 2034. The contract was a competitive acquisition, and one offer was received. Fiscal 2026 procurement funds in the amount of $955,000 are being obligated at time of award. Air Combat Command Acquisition Management and Integration Center, Patrick Space Force Base, Florida, is the contracting activity (FA7022-26-D-0006).

Blue Origin LLC, Merritt Island, Florida, has been awarded an $11,707,762 firm-fixed-price modification (P00001) to a previously awarded contract (FA2385-25-C-B023) to define requirements for how Blue Origin systems and technologies could be used for the rocket cargo mission. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $13,074,453 from $1,366,691. Work will be performed in Merritt Island, Florida; and Kent, Washington, and is expected to be completed by Oct. 29, 2027. Fiscal 2025 research, development, test and evaluation funds (Space Force) in the amount of $1,130,643; and fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds (Space Force) in the amount of $10,464,929, are being obligated at time of award. The Air Force Research Laboratory, Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio, is the contracting activity (FA2385-25-C-B023).

Omni Federal LLC, Gainesville, Virginia, has been awarded a $9,489,465 firm-fixed-price contract for the development of a front office tool for System Delta 89. This contract provides for a comprehensive solution that will be designed to automate processes, streamline workflows, and enhance overall operational efficiency. Work will be performed in Los Angeles, California, and is expected to be completed by July 31, 2029. This contract leveraged a Small Business Innovation Research Phase III Basic Ordering Agreement. Fiscal 2025 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $3,495,593 are being obligated at the time of award. The Space Combat Power Directorate, Space Systems Command, Los Angeles Air Force Base, California, is the contracting activity (FA8819-26-F-B026).

NAVY

General Dynamics Mission Systems, Taunton, Massachusetts, is awarded a $229,658,178 fixed-price incentive modification to previously awarded contract (N00024-21-C-6425) for the procurement of 188 additional Hammerhead units and eight complementary peculiar support equipment sets. Work will be performed in Taunton, Massachusetts (55%); Braintree, Massachusetts (15%); Lincoln, Nebraska (13%); Wilmington, Massachusetts (10%); and Accident, Maryland (7%), and is expected to be completed by October 2028. Fiscal 2025 weapons procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $229,658,178 will be obligated at time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract action was not competitively procured in accordance with 10 U.S. Code 3204(a)(1) (one responsible source). Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity.

Sikorsky Aircraft Corp., Lockheed Martin Co., Stratford, Connecticut, is awarded a $107,289,338 cost-plus-fixed-fee, firm-fixed-price order (N0001926F1126) against a previously issued basic ordering agreement (N0001923G0002). This order provides for the design, integration, and testing of the Power Margin Increment Two main rotor blades for the VH-92A Presidential Helicopter. This effort supports the development, integration, and qualification of an improved main rotor blade design to increase aircraft power margin and enhance operational performance. Work will be performed in Stratford, Connecticut (72%); West Palm Beach, Florida (25%); Fort Worth, Texas (2%); and King of Prussia, Pennsylvania (1%), and is expected to be completed in March 2031. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation (Navy) funds in the amount of $18,424,287 will be obligated at the time of award. This order was not competed. Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

Bird-Johnson Propeller Co. LLC, Walpole, Massachusetts, is awarded a $98,701,174 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for the commercial depot-level overhaul of Navy controllable-pitch propeller hubs and oil distribution boxes. A total amount of $362,706 will be obligated at time of award with the issuance of the first task order. Work will be performed in Walpole, Massachusetts, and is expected to be completed by July 2029. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Navy) funds in the total amount of $362,706 will be obligated at time of award and will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. All other funding will be made available at the task order level as contracting actions occur. This contract was not competitively procured in accordance with 10 U.S. Code 3204(a)(1) (only one responsible source and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements). Naval Surface Warfare Center Philadelphia Division, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, is the contracting activity (N6449826D1013).

Burns & McDonnell-Jacobs MIDLANT MC JV, Chesapeake, Virginia, is being awarded a firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity, architect-engineering contract with a maximum amount of $95,000,000 for multi-discipline architecture-engineer services in the The Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command (NAVFAC) Mid-Atlantic area of responsibility including Marine Corps Air Station (MCAS) Cherry Point, North Carolina (40%); Marine Corps Air Station New River, North Carolina (20%); Marine Corps Air Station Beaufort, South Carolina (20%); Marine Corps Base Camp Lejeune, North Carolina (10%); and Marine Recruit Depot Parris Island, South Carolina (10%). The term of the contract is not to exceed 60 months with an expected completion date of July 2031. The first task order is being awarded in the amount $10,108,938 for field investigation for four new buildings and sites located at MCAS Cherry Point, North Carolina. Fiscal 2024 military construction (Navy) funds in the amount of $10,108,938 will be obligated at the time of award and will expire at the end of fiscal 2029. Future task orders will be primarily funded by military construction (Navy) and operations and maintenance (Marine Corps) funds. This contract was competitively procured via the www.SAM.gov website, with eight offers received. NAVFAC, Mid-Atlantic, Norfolk, Virginia, is the contracting activity (N40085-26-D-00560).

NORESCO LLC, Westborough, Massachusetts, is awarded a $44,318,672 firm-fixed-price energy savings performance contract task order to a previously awarded Department of Energy indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for energy improvements at Naval Base Ventura County, California. Work will be performed in Port Hueneme, Point Mugu, and San Nicolas Island, California, and is expected to be complete by September 2028. The exact amount of the task order will be determined by financing at the time of award. No funds will be obligated at time of award. This contract was competitively procured, with fifteen offers received. Naval Facilities Engineering and Expeditionary Warfare Center, Port Hueneme, California, is the contracting activity (N39430-26-F1003).

General Dynamics One Source LLC, Falls Church, Virginia, is awarded a $43,573,376 firm-fixed-price modification (P00011) to contract N0001924C0057. This modification adds scope to procure transitional information technology and information assurance and cybersecurity services in support of the F-35 Joint Strike Fighter program. Efforts include program and performance management, enterprise architecture, documentation management, implementation of emerging capabilities and requirements, life cycle management, operations and maintenance, enterprise data management, service desk support, information technology training, application and software license management, and cybersecurity support for the F-35 Joint Strike Fighter Virtual Enterprise network and standalone workstations. These services are essential to supporting the continued fielding of F-35 mission capabilities for the Air Force, Marine Corps, Navy, Foreign Military Sales (FMS) customers, and F-35 cooperative program partners. Work will be performed in Arlington, Virginia (79.9%); Edwards Air Force Base, California (5.5%); Patuxent River, Maryland (3.7%); Fort Worth, Texas (3.1%); Eglin AFB, Florida (2.4%); China Lake, California (1.2%); Wright-Patterson AFB, Ohio (1.2%); and various locations within the continental U.S. (3%), and is expected to be completed in July 2027. Fiscal 2026 aircraft procurement (Air Force) funds in the amount of $40,431,753; fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Navy) funds in the amount of $380,836; fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Marine Corps) funds in the amount of $ $125,000; and FMS customer funds in the amount of $2,635,786, will be obligated at the time of award. This contract was not competed. Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

Damen Shipbuilding & Cooperation B.V., Gorinchem, The Netherlands, is awarded a $40,498,088 firm-fixed-price modification to a previously awarded contract (N00024-25-C-2414) to establish royalty fees for up to eight Medium Landing Ships. Work will be performed in Gorinchem, The Netherlands, and is expected to be completed by September 2027. Fiscal 2026 shipbuilding and conversion (Navy) funds in the amount of $40,498,088 will be obligated at time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Portfolio Acquisition Executive Maritime, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity.

Bell Boeing Joint Project Office, Amarillo, Texas, is awarded a not-to-exceed $35,795,938 modification (P00001) to a previously awarded firm-fixed-price, undefinitized, delivery order (N0001926F0141) under basic ordering agreement N0001922G0002. This modification increases the not-to-exceed ceiling, adding scope to procure an additional five Nacelle improvements kits and 10 shipsets of pylon support assemblies for retrofit of MV-22 and CMV-22 aircraft in support of enhancing the operational capabilities of the aircraft for the Navy and Marine Corps. Work will be performed in Amarillo, Texas (84%); Fort Worth, Texas (15%); and Ridley Park, Pennsylvania (1%), and is expected to be completed in August 2030. Fiscal 2025 aircraft procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $23,139,716; and fiscal 2026 aircraft procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $3,707,236, will be obligated at the time of award. This contract was not competed. Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

Coastal Enterprises, Jacksonville, North Carolina, is awarded a $29,535,110 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for grounds maintenance services. This contract provides services at Marine Corps Base Camp Lejeune, North Carolina; Marine Corps Air Station New River, North Carolina; and other outlying areas supported by these installations, and will be completed by July 2027. The maximum dollar value, including the base period and four option years, is $148,897,869. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Marine Corps) funding in the amount of $11,817,523; fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance, (Navy) funding in the amount of $283,985; and fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance, other funding in the amount of $410,029, will be obligated at the time of award and will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was sole source procured under Federal Acquisitions Regulation (FAR) 8.002, mandatory sources, utilizing FAR 8.7-the AbilityOne program, with one offer received. Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command, Mid-Atlantic, Norfolk, Virginia, is the contracting activity (N40085-26-D-0064).

Northrop Grumman Corp., Charlottesville, Virginia, is awarded a $18,121,584 firm-fixed-price modification to a previously awarded contract (N00024-23-C-5234) to exercise options for the manufacture of Navy and Foreign Military Sales (FMS) surface and submarine inertial sensor module configurations and associated support equipment in support of the IWS 6 Navigation program. This contract combines purchases for the Navy (23%); and the governments of Canada (44%); and Australia (33%), under the FMS program. Work will be performed in Charlottesville, Virginia (84%); Salt Lake City, Utah (14%); Woodland Hills, California (1%); and Baltimore, Maryland (1%), and is expected to be completed by February 2029. FMS (Canada) funds in the amount of $7,990,232 (44%); FMS (Australia) funds in the amount of $5,992,674 (33%); fiscal 2026 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $3,929,410 (22%); and fiscal 2025 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $209,268 (1%), will be obligated at the time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity.

The Boeing Co., Seattle, Washington, is awarded a $11,381,402 cost-plus-fixed-fee order (N0001926F1107) against a previously issued basic ordering agreement (N0001926G1002). This order is for the development, testing and delivery of aircraft loadable, black label enhanced digital flight control system software for operational level retrofit for delivered aircraft to correct deficiencies in the P-8A autopilot system that were noted in software design description developmental and operational testing. The software updates will improve or resolve altitude acquire issues, autopilot placard speed exceedance resulting from out throttle retard deficiency, pitch oscillations during airspeed climb and descent, unexpected mode switch from altitude acquire to vertical speed following speed command change, slow auto throttle response, and bank limit margin tuning. Work will be performed in Tukwila, Washington, and is expected to be completed in February 2028. Royal Australian Air Force funds in the amount of $11,381,402 will be obligated at the time of award, none of which will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract action was not competed. Naval Air Systems Command Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

Tyonek Services Overhaul Facility -Stennis LLC,* Kiln, Mississippi, is awarded a $11,064,067 modification (P00011) against a cost-plus-fixed-fee, cost reimbursable contract (N6852022C0010). This modification exercises an option to provide continued maintenance support services for aircraft, aircraft engines, and associated components and materials, including diagnostic testing performed with automated equipment, calibration, repair or replacement of damaged or unserviceable parts, components or assemblies, limited manufacture of parts, and on aircraft and equipment technical assistance to operational level activities for the Fleet Readiness Center Mid-Atlantic detachment Patuxent River, Maryland. Work will be performed in Patuxent River, Maryland, and is expected to be completed in July 2027. Fiscal 2026 working capital (Navy) funds in the amount of $11,064,067 will be obligated at the time of award. This contract was competed. Commander Fleet Readiness Center, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

Army Contracting Command, Aberdeen Proving Ground, Maryland, is the contracting activity (W9128Z-26-D-A003).

The Dutra Group, San Rafael, California (W912HY-26-D-A018); Great Lakes Dredge & Dock Co. LLC, Houston, Texas (W912HY-26-D-A019); Manson Construction Co., Seattle, Washington (W912HY-26-D-A020); and Weeks Marine Inc., Covington, Louisiana (W912HY-26-D-A021), will compete for each order of the $95,000,000 firm-fixed-price contract for hopper dredging projects within the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Galveston District boundary. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, New Orleans, Louisiana, is the contracting activity (W912P8-26-D-A007).

The Boeing Co., Mesa, Arizona, was awarded a $22,282,170 modification (P00014) to contract W58RGZ-24-C-0036 for AH-64E Apache aircraft maintenance support. Army Contracting Command, Redstone Arsenal, Alabama, is the contracting activity.

DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY

LLC, Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri, has been awarded an estimated $90,732,281 fixed-price with economic-price-adjustment, pass-through cost-for-commodity contract for operation and maintenance of the natural gas utility system at Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri. This was a sole-source acquisition using justification 10 U.S. Code 3204 (a)(1), as stated in Federal Acquisition Regulation 6.302-1. This is a 10 year contract with no option periods. The performance completion date is July 31, 2036. Using military service is Army. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2036 Army operations and maintenance funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Energy, Fort Belvoir, Virginia (SP0600-26-C-8380).

SupplyCore Inc.,* Rockford, Illinois, has been awarded a maximum $90,000,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for facilities maintenance, repair and operations supplies. This was a sole-source acquisition using justification 10 U.S. Code 3204 (a)(1), as stated in Federal Acquisition Regulation 6.302-1. This is a 487-day bridge contract with no option periods. Locations of performance are North Carolina, South Caroline, Tennessee, and Georgia, with a Dec. 2, 2027, ordering period end date. Using customers are Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, and Coast Guard. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2027 defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Troop Support, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania (SPE8E3-26-D-0012).

Valiant Technical Services Inc., Chantilly, Virginia, has been awarded an estimated $32,139,527 modification (P00008) exercising the first one-year option period of a one-year base contract (SP3300-25-C-5002) with four one-year option periods. Location of performance is Georgia, with a July 31, 2027, performance completion date. Using military service is Air Force. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Distribution, New Cumberland, Pennsylvania.

Peckham Inc.,** Lansing, Michigan, has been awarded a maximum $31,571,742 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for the aircrew endurance survival vests and components. Bethel Industries Inc.,* Jersey City, New Jersey, has been awarded a maximum $18,532,500 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for snow camouflage parkas. This was a competitive acquisition with 13 responses received. This is a five-year contract with no option periods. The ordering period end date is July 30, 2031.
