NAVY

Raytheon Missiles and Defense, Marlborough, Massachusetts, was awarded a $271,493,519 firm-fixed-price, cost-plus-fixed-fee contract for the production of AEGIS Weapon System Fire Control System MK 99 equipment, AEGIS modernization equipment, and associated engineering support. This contract includes options which, if exercised, would bring the cumulative value of this contract to $302,476,216. This contract combines purchases for the Navy (97%); and the governments of Australia (2%), and Japan (1%), under the Foreign Military Sales (FMS) program. Work will be performed in Andover, Massachusetts (56%); Chesapeake, Virginia (28%); Marlborough, Massachusetts (15%); and Burlington, Massachusetts (1%), and is expected to be completed by July 29, 2032. If all options are exercised, work will continue through December 2032. Fiscal 2025 shipbuilding and conversion (Navy) funs in the amount of $107,736,242 (40%); fiscal 2023 shipbuilding and conversion (Navy) funds in the amount of $106,781,648 (39%); fiscal 2024 shipbuilding and conversion (Navy) funds in the amount of $41,117,291 (14%); FMS (Australia) funds in the amount of $6,015,401 (2%), fiscal 2026 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $5,425,335 (2%), fiscal 2025 defense-wide procurement funds in the amount of $1,484,061 (1%); FMS (Japan) funds in the amount of $1,021,780 (1%); and fiscal 2025 other procurement (Navy) funds in the amount of $492,476 (1%), were obligated at time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was not competitively procured in accordance with 10 U.S. Code 3204(a)(1) (only one responsible source and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements). Naval Sea Systems Command, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity (N00024-26-C-5340). (Awarded July 29, 2026).

Advanced Acoustic Concepts LLC, Lemont Furnace, Pennsylvania, is awarded a $53,943,257 cost-plus-incentive-fee, cost-plus-fixed-fee, cost, and firm fixed-price contract for production of two remote mine hunting modules and accompanying engineering support. This contract includes options which, if exercised, would bring the cumulative value of this contract to $196,206,760. Work will be performed in Lemont Furnace, Pennsylvania (66%); Clarksburg, Maryland (20%); Great River, New York (7%); and Washington, D.C. (7%), and is expected to be completed by October 2029. If all options are exercised, work will continue through September 2033. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Navy) funds in the amount of $53,943,257 will be obligated at time of award and will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was competitively procured via the SAM.gov website, with three offers received. The Portfolio Acquisition Executive Maritime, Washington, D.C., is the contracting activity (N00024-26-C-6304).

EJC International Services LLC,* Wayne, New Jersey, is awarded a $50,000,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for architect-engineer services. This contract provides facility condition inspections in support of the Navy’s specialized inspection program for aircraft hangars and various types of communication and utility towers, as well as inspection and repair of select towers at military installations. Work will be performed at military installations in the U.S. and worldwide and is expected to complete by August 2031. The maximum dollar value, including the base period, is $50,000,000. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Navy) funds in the amount of $10,000 will be obligated at the time of award to satisfy the minimum guarantee and will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was competitively procured via PIEE.gov website, with one offer received. Naval Facilities Engineering and Expeditionary Warfare Center, Port Hueneme, California, is the contracting activity (N39430-26-D-7001).

InSynergy Engineering Inc., Honolulu, Hawaii, is awarded a $30,000,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for architect-engineer services for utility system studies. Work will be performed at various locations within the Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command Pacific (NAVFAC) area of responsibility including, but not limited to Japan (50%); Hawaii (10%); Guam (10%); the continental U.S. (10%); and other locations (20%), including the Philippines, Australia, Diego Garcia, and Singapore. Work is expected to be complete by August 2031. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance (Navy) funds in the amount of $10,000 are obligated for the minimum guarantee and will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. Future task orders will be primarily funded by operation and maintenance funds. This contract was competitively procured via the sam.gov website with three offers received. NAVFAC Pacific, Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam, Hawaii, is the contracting activity (N62742-26-D-1212).

Lockheed Martin Corp., Fort Worth, Texas, is awarded a not-to-exceed $20,164,487 modification (P00001) to an undefinitized firm-fixed-price order (N0001926F2171) under a previously issued basic ordering agreement (N0001924G0010). This modification adds scope to procure afloat spares packages and deployment spares packages for air vehicle initial spares in support of F-35 production aircraft for the government of the Netherlands. Work will be performed in Fort Worth, Texas, and is expected to be completed in July 2033. F-35 cooperative program partners funds in the amount of $20,164,487 will be obligated at the time of award. This contract was not competed. The Naval Air Systems Command, Patuxent River, Maryland, is the contracting activity.

CAE USA Inc., Tampa, Florida, is awarded an $11,135,132 firm-fixed-price, cost reimbursable modification (P00002). This exercises Option Year One to procure contractor operation and maintenance services, contract engineering support, courseware revision and maintenance, contractor instructor services, cyber sustainment, and configuration management to support the Royal Australian Navy MH-60R training facilities at Albatross, Nowra, New South Wales, Australia, in the Seahawk Simulation and Warfare Centre. Work will be performed in Albatross, Nowra, New South Wales, Australia, and is expected to be completed August 2027. Foreign Military Sales customer funds in the amount of $11,135,132 will be obligated at the time of the award, none of which expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was not competed. Naval Air Warfare Center Training Systems Division, Orlando, Florida, is the contracting activity (N6134025C0002).

DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY

Hutchinson Industries Inc., Trenton, New Jersey, has been awarded a maximum $191,574,900 firm-fixed-price, requirements contract for wheel and tire assemblies. This was a competitive acquisition with fourteen responses received. This is a five-year contract with no option periods. The ordering period end date is Aug. 2, 2031. Using military service is Army. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2031 Army working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Warren, Michigan (SPRDL1-26-D-0044).

Dominion Energy South Carolina Inc., Cayce, South Carolina, has been awarded an estimated $27,448,885 modification (P00073) to a 50‐year contract (SP0600‐18‐C‐8326) with no option periods to incorporate tariff rate adjustments as authorized by the South Carolina Public Service Commission. The performance completion date is Nov. 18, 2069. Using military service is Army. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2070 Army operations and maintenance funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Energy, Fort Belvoir, Virginia.

General Electric Co., Lynn, Massachusetts, has been awarded a maximum $10,222,208 modification (P00011) to a delivery order (SPE4AX-25-F-1356) issued against a five-year eight-month base contract (SPE4AX-20-D-9445) with one four-year four-month option period for an additional lot payment for July 2026 under Performance Period Five for J85 engine supplies. The performance completion date is July 31, 2026. Using military services are Air Force and Navy. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 defense appropriated funds and defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Richmond, Virginia. (Awarded on June 29, 2026).

General Electric Co., Lynn, Massachusetts, has been awarded a maximum $10,222,208 modification (P00012) to a delivery order (SPE4AX-25-F1356) issued against a five-year nine-month base contract (SPE4AX-20-D-9445) with one four-year three-month option period for an additional lot payment for August 2026 under Performance Period Five for J85 engine supplies. The performance completion date is Aug. 31, 2026. Using military services are Air Force and Navy. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 defense appropriated funds and defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Weapons Support, Richmond, Virginia. (Awarded on July 28, 2026).

ARMY

Callan Marine Ltd., Galveston, Texas, (W912HY-26-D-A022); Crosby Dredging LLC, Golden Meadow, Louisiana (W912HY-26-D-A023); Great Lakes Dredge & Dock Co. LLC, Houston, Texas (W912HY-26-D-A024); Inland Dredging Co. LLC, Dyersburg, Tennessee (W912HY-26-D-A025); Manson Construction Co., Seattle, Washington (W912HY-26-D-A026); Mike Hooks LLC, Westlake, Louisiana (W912HY-26-D-A027); Muddy Water Dredging LP, Orange, Texas (W912HY-26-D-A028); Orion Marine Construction Inc., Port Lavaca, Texas (W912HY-26-D-A029); and Weeks Marine Inc., Covington, Louisiana (W912HY-26-D-A030), will compete for each order of the $95,000,000 firm-fixed-price contract for pipeline dredging projects within the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Galveston District boundary. Bids were solicited via the internet with nine received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 3, 2031. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Galveston, Texas, is the contracting activity.

BL Harbert International LLC, Birmingham, Alabama, was awarded a firm-fixed-price contract for an F-35 Special Access Program Facility. The amount of this action is $37,607,122 with a total cumulative face value of $38,958,927. Bids were solicited via the internet with six received. Work will be performed in Fort Smith, Arkansas, with an estimated completion date of Jan. 31, 2029. Fiscal 2026 military construction, Army National Guard funds in the amount of $37,607,122 were obligated at the time of the award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Fort Worth, Texas, is the contracting activity (W9127S-26-C-A009).

Cooper Tacia General Contracting Co., Raleigh, North Carolina, was awarded a $24,500,000 firm-fixed price contract for construction of a child development center. Bids were solicited via the Internet with four received. Work will be performed in Columbia, South Carolina, with an estimated completion date of August 19, 2028. Fiscal 2026 military construction, Army funds in the amount of $24,500,000 were obligated at the time of the award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Charleston, South Carolina, is the contracting activity (W912HP-26-C-A008).

Morrish-Wallace Construction Inc.,* Cheboygan, Michigan, was awarded a $20,000,000 firm-fixed-price contract for Toledo Harbor/River dredging. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of August 2, 2028. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Buffalo, New York, is the contracting activity (W912P4-26-D-A005).

Sievert Electric Service and Sales Co., Forest Park, Illinois, was awarded a firm-fixed-price contract for Bonneville Spillway gantry crane rehabilitation. The amount of this action is $14,620,737 with a total cumulative face value of $21,627,257. Bids were solicited via the internet with two received. Work will be performed in Cascade Locks, Oregon, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 3, 2026. Fiscal 2026 Bonneville Power Administration Capital funds in the amount of $14,620,737 were obligated at the time of the award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Portland, Oregon, is the contracting activity (W9127N-26-C-A029).

AIR FORCE

Yorktown Systems Group Inc., Huntsville, Alabama, has been awarded a $51,565,132 firm-fixed-price contract for the Warfighter Training Support Services (WTSS) contract. This contract provides WTSS for the U.S. Air Force Academy. Work will be performed at the U.S. Air Force Academy, Colorado, and is expected to be complete by Aug. 2, 2031. This contract was a competitive acquisition, and 12 offers were received. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $3,510,540 are being obligated at the time of award. The 10th Contracting Squadron, U.S. Air Force Academy, Colorado, is the contracting activity (FA7000-26-C-0016).

OEI-AECOM JV2 LLC, Dallas, Texas (FA4887-26-D-0004); Spur Nicklaus JV LLC, Tempe, Arizona (FA4887-26-D-0008); and Spees-Reid Middleton JV LLC, Kent, Washington (FA4887-26-D-0009), were awarded a maximum shared ceiling $25,000,000 indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity, firm-fixed-price contract for multidisciplinary architect-engineer services. These contracts provide mechanical, electrical, fire protection, civil, structural, and architectural engineering Title I and Title II services to support various locations in Arizona including Luke Air Force Base, Davis-Monthan AFB, Barry M. Goldwater Range, Gila Bend Auxiliary Airfield, Auxiliary 1 Site, Fort Tuthill, and other related facilities. Performance locations will be determined with each task order. These contracts were competitive acquisitions, and 18 offers were received. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $7,500 ($2,500 per awardee) are being obligated at the time of award. The 56th Contracting Squadron, Luke AFB, Arizona, is the contracting activity.

Professional Contract Services Inc., Austin, Texas, has been awarded a $9,221,097 indefinite-delivery, requirements contract for base operational support management. This contract provides for all personnel, equipment and tools necessary to support all services required according to the performance work statement in support of the Special Warfare Training Group. Work will be performed at Camp Bullis, Texas, and is expected to be completed by March 31, 2031. This contract was a sole source acquisition. Fiscal 2026 operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $177,844 are being obligated at time of award. The 502nd Contracting Squadron, Joint Base San Antonio-Lackland, Texas, is the contracting activity (FA3016-26-D-0021).

*Small business
