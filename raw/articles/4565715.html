ARMY

Bering Weston JV LLC,* Anchorage, Alaska (W9128F-26-D-A038); Bristol Engineering Services Company LLC,* Anchorage, Alaska (W9128F-26-D-A039); Dawson Federal Inc.,* Honolulu, Hawaii (W9128F-26-D-A040); Goshawk LLC,* San Antonio, Texas (W9128F-26-D-A041); Jack Wayte CMS JV,* Alamogordo, New Mexico (W9128F-26-D-A042); Reliable Contracting Group LLC,* Louisville, Kentucky (W9128F-26-D-A043); and SES Energy Services LLC,* Knoxville, Tennessee (W9128F-26-D-A044), will compete for each order of the $699,000,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for design-build and design-bid-build construction services in support of the Petroleum, Oil, and Lubricants Mandatory Center of Expertise. Bids were solicited via the internet with 21 received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 6, 2031. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Omaha, Nebraska, is the contracting activity.

Teledyne FLIR Unmanned Ground Systems Inc., Chelmsford, Massachusetts, was awarded a $44,459,699 firm-fixed-price, cost-plus-fixed-fee, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for post-production software sustainment of the Man Transportable Robotic System Increment II, including software maintenance, engineering support, systems technical support, and sustainment systems technical support throughout the system lifecycle. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 6, 2031. Army Contracting Command, Detroit Arsenal, Michigan, is the contracting activity (W912CH-26-D-A106).

GEM Building Contractors & Developers Inc., New Castle, Pennsylvania, was awarded a firm-fixed-price contract for design-bid-build construction of an area maintenance support activity and vehicle maintenance shop. The amount of this action is $26,080,966 with a total cumulative face value of $26,122,719. Bids were solicited via the internet with eight received. Work will be performed in New Castle, Pennsylvania, with an estimated completion date of Oct. 16, 2028. Fiscal 2026 military construction, Army Reserve funds in the amount of $26,080,966 were obligated at the time of the award. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Louisville, Kentucky, is the contracting activity (W912QR-26-C-A024).

Luxfer Magtech Inc., Cincinnati, Ohio, was awarded a $10,078,777 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for procurement of the M256A2 Chemical Agent Detector Kit. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work locations and funding will be determined with each order, with an estimated completion date of Aug. 6, 2031. Army Contracting Command, Detroit Arsenal, Michigan, is the contracting activity (W912CH-26-D-0048).

NAVY

Rogers, Lovelock & Fritz Inc., Orlando, Florida, is awarded an indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity, architect-engineering contract with a maximum amount of $127,000,000 for multi-discipline projects at various activities in the Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command Northwest area of responsibility. The contract’s original ceiling is increased by $27,000,000 to execute new task orders for mission-critical requirements. Work will be performed at various government installations in, but not limited to, Washington (75%), Alaska (22%), Idaho (1%), Montana (1%), and Oregon (1%). Work is expected to be completed by September 2026. Task orders will be primarily funded by fiscal 2026 military construction (Navy) funds. This contract was competitively procured via the Navy Electronic Commerce Online website with four proposals received. Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command Northwest, Silverdale, Washington, is the contracting activity (N44255-20-D-0002).

World Wide Technology LLC, St. Louis, Missouri, is awarded a $14,897,521 firm-fixed-price contract for Operational Command Post – Heavy hardware and licenses. This contract provides modular, expeditionary information technology infrastructure to Fleet Marine Forces. This scalable architecture enables the hosting and rapid deployment of secure, enterprise-grade services at the tactical edge within disconnected, intermittent, and limited environments. Work will be performed at St. Louis, Missouri, with an expected completion date of Dec. 3, 2026. The maximum dollar value is $14,897,521 with no option years. Fiscal 2026 procurement (Marine Corps) funds in the amount of $14,897,521 are being obligated at time of award. Contract funds in the amount of $14,897,521 will not expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was competitively procured via NASA Solutions for Enterprise-Wide, and two offers were received. Marine Corps Systems Command, Quantico, Virginia, is the contracting activity (M67854-26-F-0135).

Dragoon Technology LLC,* Tucson, Arizona, is awarded a $9,879,151 firm-fixed-price contract for non-recurring engineering activities to integrate energetics into the internally developed Coriolis Unmanned Aerial System (UAS), as well as support multiple test and evaluation events focused on assessing the operational suitability and performance of the Coriolis UAS with onboard collaborative autonomy for multiple systems under Small Business Innovation Research Phase II Topic A234-P017, entitled Engineer Operations, through the Office of the Secretary of War Accelerated Research for Transition program. Work will be performed in Tucson, Arizona (97%); and Tampa, Florida (3%), and is expected to be completed in August 2028. Fiscal 2025 research, development, test and evaluation (Defense Wide) funds in the amount of $8,999,945 will be obligated at the time of award. This contract action was competed via other than full and open competition; one firm solicited, and one offer received. Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division, Lakehurst, New Jersey, is the contracting activity.

DEFENSE LOGISTICS AGENCY

MM Manufacturing LLC,* Lajas, Puerto Rico, has been awarded a maximum $31,151,250 fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for improved hot weather combat uniform female coats. This was a competitive acquisition with seven offers received. This is a five-year contract with no option periods. The ordering period end date is Aug. 5, 2031. Using military services are Army, Air Force, and Space Force. Type of appropriation is fiscal 2026 through 2031 defense working capital funds. The contracting activity is Defense Logistics Agency Troop Support, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania (SPE1C1-26-D-0050).

AIR FORCE

The Boeing Co., El Segundo, California, has been awarded a $22,630,769 cost-plus-fixed-fee modification (P00023) to previously awarded contract FA880725CB006 for Evolved Strategic Satellite Communications Space Development and Production. The modification brings the total cumulative face value of the contract to $2,869,121,687 from $2,846,490,918. Work will be performed at El Segundo, California, and is expected to be completed by Feb. 1, 2026. Fiscal 2026 research, development, test and evaluation funds in the amount of $22,630,769 are being obligated at time of award. The Space Systems Command Satellite Communications and Positioning, Navigation and Timing, Los Angeles Air Force Base, California, is the contracting activity.

Environmental Abatement Inc., Layton, Utah, has been awarded a ceiling $9,900,000 firm-fixed-price, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract for abatement services. This contract provides for removal of asbestos, lead-based paint, heavy metals, animal excrement, and other abatement and sampling and inspection activities. Work will be performed at Hill Air Force Base, Utah, and other geographically separated areas under its jurisdiction, and is expected to be completed by Jan. 19, 2034. This contract was a competitive acquisition, and two offers were received. Fiscal 2025 operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $250 are being obligated at time of award. The Air Force Sustainment Center, Hill AFB, Utah, is the contracting activity.

Shipcom Federal Solutions LLC, Houston, Texas, has been awarded a $9,287,035 firm-fixed-price contract for eight artificial intelligence test program sets. This contract provides for artificial intelligence test program sets in strict accordance with the performance work statement. Work will be performed at Hill Air Force Base, Utah, and is expected to be completed by Aug. 5, 2028. This contract is a Small Business Innovation Research Phase III direct award. Fiscal 2025 procurement funds in the amount of $9,287,035 are being obligated at the time of award. The Air Force Sustainment Center, Hill AFB, Utah, is the contracting activity (FA822826-C-0007). (Awarded on Aug. 4, 2026)

*Small business
